# Intent: Agent turn summaries spoken by Nixi, with one voice and his face

## Problem

When Claude Code, Codex or agy finishes a turn, `agent-say`
(nixos_config `modules/programs/agent-voice.nix`, `pkgs/agent-say`) speaks a
short summary in ElevenLabs "Lily". Nixi knows nothing about it:

- **Two voices.** `agent-say` and Nixi have separate speech paths. They sound
  different, and they can talk over each other: `agent-say` only locks against
  itself.
- **No face for the speech.** His finished alert shows a happy face, a chime and
  a line like `✓ Claude finished · myproject`, but it is silent and separate. It
  fires when the turn ends, and the spoken summary comes later. His face and
  captions don't follow the summary.
- **Little control while running.** Each agent can only be turned off by
  rebuilding NixOS or per session. At run time there is one on/off switch and
  "tell me more". Nothing stops a summary from Nixi or replays an earlier one.
- **Lost summaries.** Two turns finishing together can lose one: the second is
  skipped by the lock, and a shared `last-<agent>.json` can be overwritten.

## Proposed outcome

**When Nixi is running and shown,** a finished turn gives visual and voice
feedback together:

- he says the summary in the same voice as everything else he says, through
  his own speech queue, so it never overlaps his other speech;
- his face and captions follow it, and the existing badge shows which agent
  finished and in which project;
- it never opens the microphone afterwards;
- the usual ways of stopping him stop it.

**When Nixi isn't running,** `agent-say` speaks as it does today, so no summary
is missed on a host without him.

**When you hid Nixi yourself** (Esc, or hide in the menu), nothing is spoken and
the old voice doesn't step in: hiding him means quiet.

**When he only hid himself because he was idle,** he is still awake. He pops back
up and speaks, as he already does for agent alerts.

**When Do Not Disturb, quiet mode, a call or a meeting applies,** nothing is
spoken. If one starts during a summary, he stops after the current sentence, as
`agent-say` does today. A summary still waiting in his queue is checked again
before it plays.

**When Nixi is running but can't say it** (daily cap reached, or the speech was
stopped), it stays visual. The old voice is only for when he isn't running at all.

**One finished alert per turn.** The spoken summary and today's finished alert
act as one for turns long enough to be summarised: no second badge or chime, and
no separate "tell me more" notification while he is the one speaking. Short turns
keep today's silent alert.

**Stopping him stops only the speech,** not any work he is doing for you.

**More control, in Settings:**

- mute each agent separately (Claude, Codex, agy);
- replay the last few summaries, held in memory only and gone when he restarts;
- "tell me more" keeps working, with its key and from Nixi. `agent-say` makes the
  longer version, because only it holds the reply.

## Affected users and systems

- Fork `olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake`: a new narrowly authorised way to hand
  Nixi a summary, the speech queue, the badge and Settings.
- `nixos_config`: `pkgs/agent-say` (try Nixi, then fall back) and
  `modules/programs/agent-voice.nix`.
- p620 and razer. p510 has no companion and isn't affected.
- Any host running `agent-say` without Nixi keeps today's behaviour.

## Constraints

- **Must:** not a folder plugin. Plugins are sandboxed, can't reach localhost
  and are killed after 8 s at most, so this is a built-in feature.
- **Must:** hooks, summariser choice, keys and per-host settings stay in NixOS.
  `agent-say` stays the hook runner and keeps its silence checks.
- **Must:** never two voices for one turn. A turn is spoken by Nixi or by the
  fallback, never both.
- **Must:** the hand-off uses a limited token or path, never Nixi's full-access
  window token in a hook or a plugin.
- **Must:** only the generated summary reaches Nixi, never the raw reply or the
  transcript. Replay holds a few summaries in memory and nothing on disk, so
  "Nothing the agents say is saved or logged" stays true.
- **Must:** a muted agent or an active silence check should not cost a summariser
  call where it can be avoided. Today the summary is written first.
- **Must:** the Stop hook still returns quickly (10 s limit), so nothing waits on
  Nixi or the summariser inside the hook.
- **Must:** the same voice depends on the `speak()` change on
  `fix/176-same-lily`, which lands first.
- **Must not:** speak on events other than a finished turn.
- Rollback is one setting per host, without touching the hooks.

## Open questions

1. **Summaries and the ElevenLabs daily cap.** Recommended: they count against
   Nixi's cap once he speaks them, and when the cap is reached the summary stays
   visual rather than switching to another voice.
2. **Two agents finishing close together.** Recommended: queue them, up to a
   small limit, so each is heard once; beyond the limit the oldest isn't spoken
   but can still be replayed. This includes two sessions of the same agent.
3. **Default.** Recommended: on for p620 and razer once checked live, as one
   NixOS option per host.
4. **Replay and per-agent mute in this issue, or later?** Both reviewers note they
   are separate from the core voice-and-face outcome, and agy would cut them.
   Recommended: keep them, since you asked for more control, and build them last
   so they can be dropped without touching the rest.

idle-hide rule as written.
