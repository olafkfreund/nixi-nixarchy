# Intent: Nixi dresses for the weather, and it looks good

Code: the companion (`nixoarchy-snowflake`): `app/static/index.html` for the
drawing, and `app/server.py` for the weather data. Artifacts: this repo.

## Problem

- **The rain umbrella doesn't read as an umbrella.** The user's screenshot
  on p620 shows a small, flat yellow shape floating above his head. Codex
  and agy both reviewed the code, and their findings are confirmed:
  - it's drawn at 75% (`index.html:941`): its canopy is about 81 units
    across. That's narrower than his headphones (about 168) and even than
    his pair of eyes (about 102);
  - the handle ends in mid-air above his eyes: no lambda tip holds it;
  - it never sways. The nightcap and party hat follow his motion; the
    umbrella is placed with no rotation (`put(SIT_P.um,um,0,-84)`,
    `:1511`);
  - it's one flat yellow path, with no ribs, underside or depth. It's
    drawn entirely in front of him, so nothing can pass behind his head;
  - the four drops are 6 px long and shrunk with the canopy. They fall
    straight down although they're drawn slanted, and they jump back to the
    top with no fade (`:944`, `:1512`).
- **Snow has no look of its own.** He gets excited once when snow starts
  (`:1817`), but nothing falls.
- **Most of the weather is thrown away.** Every 30 minutes the server reads
  `omarchy weather status` (e.g. "Temp 12°C · Wind ↙17km/h") and `omarchy
  weather icon`. It then reduces them to rain or snow, and cold (below
  0 °C) or hot (above 28 °C) (`server.py:1275-1284`).
  - The wind and the kind of weather (drizzle, heavy rain, thunder, cloud,
    clear) are lost, and there's no "feels like" temperature at all. So he
    can't dress for the cold or lean into the wind.
  - Night is not lost: a separate clock-based signal already covers it
    (`server.py:1378`).
- **His frame loop ignores reduced motion.** `REDUCED` is only used by the
  answer cards (`index.html:1973`, `:2046`), so today's drops fall even
  when the user asks for less motion.
- **Changing the place doesn't refresh the weather.** A new place clears
  the location cache, but the weather waits for its next 30-minute poll.

## Proposed outcome

Delivered in slices, each one shippable and useful on its own.

1. **Slice 1: rain and snow that look right, with today's data.**
   - In the rain, he **holds** an umbrella:
     - a proper size, with some depth;
     - its handle held by one of his lambda tips;
     - swaying and tilting as he moves;
     - with clearly visible, angled rain that fades in and out instead of
       jumping.
   - In snow, a few big flakes drift down past him, with no umbrella.
2. **Slice 2: he dresses for the cold and reacts to wind.**
   - The weather comes from a forecast for the user's chosen place: the
     "feels like" temperature, the kind of weather, wind and day/night.
   - **Cold:** a scarf, and an occasional small shiver.
   - **Very cold:** a beanie as well (when his head is free).
   - **Windy:** he leans into the wind, and his hat, umbrella and scarf tilt
     with it.
   - **Heavy rain** has more and faster rain.
   - Outfits don't flicker on and off around a threshold.
   - Changing the place updates his outfit within a minute.
   - The existing frost and thaw cue on his tips stays as it is.
3. **Later, not on this branch:**
   - sun, moon and clouds, folded into the sky already planned in #144
     slice 3;
   - thunder (a lightning mark and a startled look);
   - hot-and-sunny sunglasses;
   - mittens.
   Each needs its own fit and slot check, and none is needed to fix what the
   user saw.

In every slice, he still looks like Nixi. The six lambda tips and his eyes
stay visible. There's one weather item on his head at a time, and no pile of
clothes.

## Affected users and systems

- **Everyone with three switches on**, all off by default
  (`server.py:1377`):
  - "Desktop companion" (`proactive`);
  - "Weather look" (`ambient_weather`);
  - "Dress for the weather" (`look_weather`).
  Their Settings descriptions (`index.html:698`, `:713`) change to match.
- **The companion page:**
  - `drawSit()` (umbrella, drops) and its placement in `frame()`;
  - the top-zone priority, party hat > umbrella > nightcap, which applies
    only without a skin;
  - the frost/thaw cue.
- **From slice 2, the companion server:**
  - the weather fetch in `Ambient`, and the `situation` and `ambient`
    events it sends;
  - the weather quips (`Nudge`, `server.py:1644`), which read the same
    value;
  - the location API (`location_act`), which must trigger a refresh.
- **Hosts:** p620 and razer.

## Constraints

- **GPU safety (a hard rule on p620).**
  - No `backdrop-filter`, `feTurbulence`, displacement filters, WebGL or
    canvas shaders, and no blur animated per frame.
  - Few particles, moved by transform and opacity only.
  - Previews run only in headless Chromium with the GPU off, followed by a
    `journalctl -k` check, and there's a live check on p620 after deploy.
- **Everything fits his 340×460 window** through his biggest moves (the
  existing `accessories_fit` test). Nothing covers his eyes or the caption
  area.
- **Reduced motion:** outfits and still marks stay; falling particles,
  shivers, leans and tilts stop.
- **Low CPU and battery.** He runs all day, and lowFx mode still applies.
- **Slots don't fight:**
  - a skin, including a plugin hat, still blocks the top zone;
  - the top zone order becomes party hat > umbrella > beanie > nightcap,
    so he never wears a beanie under an umbrella;
  - the meeting tie wins over the scarf, and so does a plugin prop
    anchored at his base.
- **His motion, not the rain's.** Falling rain and snow must not spin or
  squash when he rolls or bounces. Nothing new shows in the notch, hover
  or calm modes.
- **Privacy (slice 2).** A forecast request sends the current place's
  coordinates (the chosen Now place, else Home) to the forecast service.
  - It's only made with all three switches on.
  - With no place that has coordinates, nothing new is sent: it falls back
    to `omarchy weather`, which already runs today.

## Open questions

1. **Slice 2's data source:** Open-Meteo with the current place
   (recommended by Codex and agy), or keep `omarchy weather` and parse more
   of its text? My suggestion: Open-Meteo, with `omarchy weather` as the
   fallback when no place has coordinates.
2. **Temperature bands:** a scarf at "feels like" ≤ 5 °C and a beanie at
   ≤ −5 °C, each with a 2 °C margin (Codex), or a scarf from < 7 °C (agy)?
   My suggestion: 5 °C and −5 °C, with the margin.
3. **Poll interval:** keep 30 minutes, or 15 (agy)? My suggestion: keep 30,
   plus a refresh when the place changes. He already refreshes when he
   reappears.
4. **This branch covers slices 1 and 2.** The "later" list stays open on
   this issue. OK?
