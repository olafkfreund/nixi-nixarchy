# Intent: News clips with previews in the news slip, played in mpv

## Problem

"Show me the latest world news" answers with the news slip (#167): up to five
stories with a source, an age and an Open button that opens the article in the
browser. The slip has no video. The user wants YouTube news clips in the same
list, each with a preview picture, and a way to play one directly in mpv (or a
similar player) instead of a browser tab.

Nixi already has the parts, but only on the cinema set: a YouTube search
through `yt-dlp` on the server (`stages.video_search`), posters fetched by the
server and handed to the page as tokens, and a Play action that starts `mpv`
(or the browser without it). The news plugin can't search YouTube itself. It
runs in the sandbox without `yt-dlp`.

## Proposed outcome

- Asking for news shows the stories first, as fast as today. A "Watch"
  section with up to three recent YouTube clips on the same topic appears
  below them when the search returns. Each clip shows a preview picture, its
  title, channel and length.
- Pressing a clip's Play starts it in mpv (in the browser if mpv isn't
  installed). Voice can tell clips and stories apart: "play the first video"
  (or "clip") plays a clip; "open the first one" still opens a story.
- The clips are recent: newest first, not the most-watched clip of all time.
- The topic follows a known word in the request: "world news" finds world
  news, "tech news" technology news. Anything else, including a bare "news",
  uses the default topic from the news settings file. What the user said is
  never sent to YouTube as is.
- If the search fails or finds nothing, the stories stay as they are and no
  Watch section appears.
- Clips can be turned off in the news settings file. Off means no YouTube
  search and no preview downloads.

## Affected users and systems

- Fork `olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake`:
  - `app/server.py`: the clip search after a news slip is shown, and voice;
  - `app/stages.py`: `headlines` may carry clips; voice parsing for "video";
  - `app/static/index.html`: the press set's Watch section;
  - the `news` plugin: topic words and the clips switch in its settings.
- p620 and razer, which run the companion. Both have `mpv` and `yt-dlp`
  (checked 2026-11-04).

## Constraints

- **Must:** adding clips updates the slip on screen in place. Story Open and
  Copy keep working while clips arrive, ← still goes to the answer before the
  news, and voice keeps working. If the slip on screen has changed by then,
  the clips are dropped, and a late result never brings an old slip back.
- **Must:** the companion page never contacts YouTube. The server searches and
  downloads the previews, and the page only gets tokens. Opening a clip in the
  browser (only without mpv) is, of course, YouTube.
- **Must:** the plugin stays as sandboxed as it is. The plugin doesn't search;
  the server does, with the existing search.
- **Must:** the agent's own `<<stage>>` block can't add clips, because
  `headlines` stays plugin-only.
- **Must:** settings say plainly that News searches YouTube when clips are on,
  for a Nix-installed plugin too, which never shows a consent card.
- **Must not:** play anything without a press or a spoken "play".
- **Must not:** run more than one clip search at a time.
- Reduced motion, contrast ≥ 4.5:1, Tab to every Play, and Esc all behave as
  on the rest of the press slip. Clips arriving count as new content for the
  30 s collapse.

## Open questions

1. **Where the topic comes from.** Recommended: a short list of topic words in
   the news settings file (world, UK, tech, business, science, sport), each
   mapped to a search ("tech" → "technology news"). The plugin returns the
   chosen search with its stories, so only listed searches reach YouTube. A
   request with no listed word uses the default, "world news".
2. **How many clips.** Recommended: three, newest first. The slip grows by
   about three 64 px rows.
3. **On by default?** Recommended: on, because it's what you asked for, with
   `"videos": false` in `news.json` to turn it off. Settings shows "Searches
   YouTube for news clips" under News.
4. **Only on the news slip?** Recommended: yes. Other plugins can't ask for
   clips until one needs them.
