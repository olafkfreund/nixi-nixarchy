# Intent: Choose a different body for Nixi: terminal, robot, devil

## Problem

Nixi always has one body: a dark glass hexagon with a snowflake inside
(`index.html`, `#body`: `path.ball`, `g.ball.nix`, the rim and the gloss). The
eyes (`#face`: `#eL`, `#eR`) are drawn on top of it and moved by code for
blinking, gaze, moods, talking (they swell with the voice) and listening.

You can recolour him and give him a "Skin", but today's skins are
**accessories** worn on the snowflake: halo, crown, sprout, bow
(`SKIN_LABELS`). Nothing changes what he *is*. The user wants other
characters to choose from:

- a small, cute **terminal** with eyes;
- a cute **robot**;
- a cute **devil**.

## Proposed outcome

- **A "Character" choice in Settings → Look**, with a live preview of each:
  Snowflake (today's Nixi, the default), Terminal, Robot and Devil.
- **Choosing one replaces his whole body.** Everything that makes him Nixi
  keeps working on every character:
  - the same eyes: blink, gaze, moods, squint, swell while talking,
    listening, sleep;
  - the user's eye and body colours;
  - his accessories (halo, crown…) where they fit;
  - weather and situation props (umbrella, scarf, party hat…);
  - his stage cards, tooltips and click area.
- **Each character has one touch of its own,** alive but never distracting:
  - Terminal: a prompt cursor blinking on its screen;
  - Robot: an antenna light that pulses;
  - Devil: a tail that swishes.

  These calm down when he is idle for a while, and stop under reduced
  motion.
- **More characters can come later as plugins**, the same way plugins already
  add props, without changing Nixi's code.

## Affected users and systems

- Fork `olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake`:
  - `app/static/index.html`: the body drawing, the animation code that
    assumes the hexagon (rim, gloss, the fit of props, the shadow), and the
    Look tab;
  - `app/server.py`: the `look` setting, a new `character` key and its
    validation;
  - possibly the plugin manifest (`plugins.py`), if characters can come from
    plugins.
- Everyone who uses the companion, on p620 and razer.

## Constraints

- **Must:** the eyes stay one shared, code-driven face. A character supplies
  only its body (and where the eyes sit). The mood, gaze, talk and blink
  logic isn't duplicated per character.
- **Must:** Snowflake stays the default and looks exactly as today.
- **Must:** reduced motion, contrast and the click-through area work on every
  character. His on-screen footprint stays within today's 340 × 460 surface.
- **Must:** performance stays as today. The animation loop already watches
  for slow frames ("slow frames — glow off"); characters mustn't add
  constant heavy filters.
- **Must:** original artwork only. No copies of existing mascots or logos
  (the terminal isn't any vendor's logo; the robot and devil are our own).
- If characters can come from plugins, the same consent and hash rules apply
  as for props, and plugin artwork is shown as an image, never as markup
  that could run.

## Open questions

1. **Setting or plugins?** Recommended: built-in characters as a setting
   (Terminal, Robot, Devil ship with Nixi), plus the option for plugins to add
   more later through the existing props mechanism, extended with a body
   slot.
2. **How do accessories and props fit other bodies?** Recommended: each
   character declares a few anchor points (top of head, face centre, base),
   and the existing accessories attach there. Any that don't fit a
   character are hidden on it, not drawn oddly.
3. **Does the character change his voice?** Recommended: no, not
   automatically. The voice stays the user's choice (#176). A character can
   *suggest* one in Settings, e.g. Robot → the Robot voice.
4. **Mockups:** Codex and agy each drew all three characters as animated
   mockups. **Decided by the user: agy's direction for all three.** That is
   the dark retro CRT terminal with a green screen, a `>▮` prompt and a
   keyboard base; the dark round helmet robot with a glass visor and a
   pulsing blue antenna; and the glossy crimson imp with golden horns, small
   wings, a fang and a swishing tail. Kept in
   `mockups/178/agy-characters.{html,png}` with agy's notes.
