# Intent: news no longer calls a slow feed unreachable

Code lives in `olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake`, read at `7efdc88`. All
measurements are from p620.

## Problem

When `hnrss.org/frontpage` is slow, the news slip says "1 couldn't be reached".
The feed usually does answer, just late, though one round below got no answer
in 15 s. The source line still lists every configured feed
(`plugins/examples/news/run:168`), so the slip doesn't say which one failed.

"Couldn't be reached" is also wider than lateness: the same count covers bad or
oversized content (`run:115`) and invalid settings entries (`run:143-150,158`).
If every feed misses the deadline, the card says "No news feeds could be
reached." (`run:162`).

The issue names the 1.5 s socket timeout at `run:110`, but a second limit sits
behind it: `run:141` gives all feeds one shared 1.5 s deadline, and `main` stops
waiting at `run:155-156`. Raising line 110 alone changes nothing.

### Measured

**curl, 20 rounds, 15 s apart, evening of 2026-11-04:**

| Feed | Median | Slowest | Over 1.5 s |
| ---- | ------ | ------- | ---------- |
| BBC News | 0.04 s | 0.12 s | 0 of 20 |
| The Guardian | 0.04 s | 0.06 s | 0 of 20 |
| hnrss.org/frontpage | 0.40 s | no answer in 15 s | 7 of 20 |
| news.ycombinator.com/rss (not a default) | 0.42 s | 0.45 s | 0 of 20 |

The hnrss delay was in the TCP connect (up to 7.2 s); once connected it took
about 0.3 s. The cause is unknown and no other network was tried.

**The plugin itself, 20 rounds, 15 s apart, 2026-11-05 07:52–07:57 UTC:** the
unchanged `run` with the default feeds, a new process each round, outside the
sandbox and with no server cache.

- It reported "1 couldn't be reached" in 1 of 20 rounds. One trial run just
  before the series also dropped; it isn't counted.
- 14 rounds took 0.47–0.54 s. The other 6 took 1.48–1.57 s, about 1 s longer.
  These are whole-process times and the deadline starts inside the process, so
  how close the 5 that reported no failure came wasn't measured.
- The plugin reports a count, not a name, so "it was hnrss" is inferred from the
  curl rounds.
- Stalls were about as common in both runs: 6 of 20 here, 8 of 20 over 1 s with
  curl. The drops differ (1 against 7) because this morning's stalls were all
  about 1 s, right at the limit, while the evening's ran up to 7.6 s or more. The
  runs are 9 hours apart, so that may be time of day rather than method.

### What limits the fix

Waiting longer has a cost: the request blocks on the plugin
(`app/server.py:5187`), then the slip goes out (`:5198`) and the stories are
spoken (`:6539`). Every extra second spent on a late feed is silence before Nixi
answers. It applies only when the server cache misses (`cache_seconds` 300,
`plugin.json:14`; keyed per phrase, locale and settings, `app/plugins.py:784-792`),
and only when a feed is late.

The wait also has a ceiling. `run_stage` defaults to `TIME_DEFAULT`, 3 s
(`app/plugins.py:501`), and neither caller passes more (`app/server.py:5187`,
`:5279`). At 3 s the server kills the whole process group (`app/plugins.py:874`)
and every feed is lost, leaving the last good result marked "couldn't refresh"
if there is one (`:803`). The sandbox added about 0.26 s when measured on
2026-11-04, which leaves about 2.7 s for feeds with nothing to spare.

Share of the 20 curl rounds for hnrss that each wait would have caught:

| Wait | Caught | Needs |
| ---- | ------ | ----- |
| 1.5 s (today) | 13 of 20 | nothing |
| 2.5 s | 14 of 20 | both plugin limits raised |
| 5 s | 18 of 20 | also a longer server limit and a new manifest key (`STAGE_KEYS`, `app/plugins.py:29`) |
| 8 s | 19 of 20 | same, at `TIME_MAX` |

A drop also costs a story. Hacker News was third of five, so spoken, in one
2026-11-04 snapshot, and in none of the top five in the 2026-11-05 rounds. A
partial result stays cached with its note for up to 5 minutes.

## Proposed outcome

- A default feed that is up isn't reported as "couldn't be reached".
- When a feed is late or fails, the slip names it and says only what is known.
- Asking for the news doesn't get noticeably slower: rounds with no late feed
  stay as fast as they are now.
- p620 gets the first point only once its own `news.json` changes (question 5).
  Until then it keeps hnrss and gets only the better wording.

## Affected users and systems

- Anyone using the news plugin with the default feeds, on any host.
- `plugins/examples/news/` in `nixoarchy-snowflake`: `run`, `defaults.json`,
  `README.md` (states 1.5 s), and the tests that pin the wording:
  `tests/test_news_plugin.py:110-111`, `tests/test_plugin_sandbox.py:412-413`,
  `tests/stages_fit.mjs:51,420`.
- Existing settings files. Pressing Edit in Settings runs `edit_config`
  (`app/server.py:1923`, `app/plugins.py:225`), which writes a full copy of the
  defaults, and that `feeds` wins over a new default (`app/plugins.py:222`).
  p620's `news.json` lists hnrss.

## Constraints

- The sandbox and consent model stay as they are (#149). Changing the plugin's
  files asks for consent again; that's expected.
- Must not change `TIME_DEFAULT` for all plugins: it bounds every stage plugin.
- The default feeds must never reach the server's 3 s kill.
- Must not overwrite anyone's feed settings.
- Must not add a dependency. The plugin uses only the standard library.
- The new wording must keep the 120-character `source` limit (`run:172`) with
  up to 8 feeds of 40 characters each. Today's trim at `run:170` goes wrong once
  the note passes 117 characters, and `app/plugins.py:803` cuts a stale `source`
  to 60.

## Open questions

1. **What happens to Hacker News?** Recommended: make
   `news.ycombinator.com/rss` the default. It needs no extra wait, took at most
   0.45 s in all 20 rounds, and carries `pubDate` on its 30 items (one fetch,
   2026-11-05). The evidence is one evening, and its rate limits and terms
   haven't been checked.
2. **How long may a late feed delay the answer?** Recommended: keep 1.5 s. It
   costs nothing, and the swap removes the reason to wait.
3. **What should the slip say?** Recommended: name the feed, for example "Hacker
   News didn't answer in time" for a timeout, and "couldn't be read" for any
   other failure. Both limits are 1.5 s, so "thread still running" alone won't
   tell them apart; `fetch` has to record the timeout itself.
4. **Should caching change, either a short cache for slow feeds or a shorter
   life for partial results?** Recommended: no. Both need a new server feature
   (`/tmp` and `/plugin` don't keep anything between runs), and the swap makes
   them unnecessary.
5. **Who updates existing settings files, including p620's?** Recommended: each
   owner, by hand; this task won't touch them. Delete `feeds` from `news.json`
   to follow the defaults, and have the README say so. Those files keep hnrss
   and its drops until then.
