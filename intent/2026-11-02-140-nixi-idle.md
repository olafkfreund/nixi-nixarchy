# Intent: Nixi shrinks when idle and pops up when something happens

Code lives in `olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake`. Artifacts live here.

## Problem

- **He always takes the same space.** Nixi is a 340×460 overlay
  (`app/window.py:118`). While you work and nothing is happening, he still
  covers that corner of the screen. With click-through off (the default), the
  whole transparent box also catches your clicks.
- **Hiding him switches him off.** Today, hiding means asleep:
  - `window.nixiHidden` posts `/api/sleep` (`index.html:2585`), which sets
    `Wake.asleep` (`server.py:4109`);
  - that closes the mic and turns off "Hey Nixi";
  - it releases the notification takeover (`Inbox.want()`, `server.py:1472`),
    so the shell shows its own popups;
  - agent alerts become plain desktop notifications.

  So he can't get out of the way **and** still tell you something.
- **Popping back up would take your keyboard.** The only way back today is
  `show()` (`window.py:756`), which calls `present()` and `grab_focus()`.
  That is right when you call him, but wrong when he pops up by himself
  while you're typing.
- **There's nothing in between.** The notch (drag him to an edge) and hover
  mode (a dot on the cursor) are manual modes for other purposes. Nothing
  makes him smaller by itself, then full size again when something happens.

## Proposed outcome

- **Idle shrink.** After a short idle time he shrinks into a small "peek" in
  his corner. Only that small peek catches clicks; the rest of his box lets
  clicks through.
- **Back to full size when something happens**, then back to the peek after
  a short hold:
  - you call him: "Hey Nixi", push-to-talk, a click or hovering over the
    peek;
  - an approval or password prompt;
  - an agent's "needs you", or a reminder;
  - a notification or a "finished" alert.

  In fullscreen and in meetings, the existing quiet rules still apply.
  Weather and look changes never wake him.
- **Optional second stage, "doze".** After a longer idle time he disappears
  completely but stays awake. The wake word and the notification takeover
  keep working, and he pops up like a notification **without taking the
  keyboard**.
- **Hide still means off.** Hiding him on purpose (Esc, the menu,
  `nixi-companion hide`) stays fully off, as today.
- **One setting with an off switch.** The user picks off, shrink only, or
  shrink then doze, and can change the timings.
- **No new flicker.** He shrinks inside the existing window, the way hover
  mode already does (`body.hover #stage{transform:scale(.17)}`,
  `index.html:26`). The window itself is not resized, which is known to
  flicker on NVIDIA (`window.py:934`).

## Affected users and systems

- `nixoarchy-snowflake`:
  - `app/static/index.html`: the idle states, which events restore him,
    the shrink animation and the click region (`sendRegion`, `:2614-2638`);
  - `app/window.py`: a quiet show that doesn't take focus, and pausing the
    redraw while dozing;
  - `app/server.py`: the new settings, `/api/state` and the allow-list;
  - `docs/GUIDE.md` and `tests/`.
- Everyone running Nixi. The default is decided in the spec, see the open
  questions.

## Constraints

- **Never steal focus.** An automatic pop-up never takes the keyboard. Only
  your own commands and required prompts (approval, password) may.
- **Never eat clicks.** While he's in the peek or dozing, the empty part of
  his window must let clicks through, whatever the click-through setting says.
- **Hide stays off.** Doze must not change what a deliberate hide does.
- **Privacy rules from #133/#138 stay the same:** quiet in meetings and
  fullscreen, notification text gated as today.
- **No resizing of the window** (the NVIDIA flicker). No new dependencies.
- **Works with the other modes:** the notch, hover mode, `step_aside`,
  follow-focus and multiple monitors keep working. Idle shrink does not apply
  in the notch or hover modes.
- **Saves CPU while dozing:** the animation and the window redraw pause while
  he's fully hidden.

## Open questions

1. **Default.** Codex recommends shrink only (on by default). agy
   recommends shrink then doze (on by default). My recommendation: **shrink
   only by default**, with doze available in Settings until it has been
   tested on NVIDIA and multiple monitors.
2. **Timings.** Proposed:
   - shrink after **45 s** idle;
   - doze after **5 min**;
   - after an event, **12 s** at full size (**20 s** for "needs you" and
     prompts).
3. **Peek size and spot.** Codex suggests about 45 %, agy about 38 %. The
   proposal is **40 %**, tucked toward the screen corner nearest to him.
4. **Mic in the peek.** Should "Hey Nixi" keep listening while he's in the
   peek and while he dozes? Proposed: **yes**, following the existing
   wake-word setting. Only a deliberate hide closes the mic.
5. **Delivery.** Proposed: one PR, after #138's PR B, because both edit the
   same parts of `index.html`.
