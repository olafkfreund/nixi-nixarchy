# Intent: the phone in Nixi, through Flux

## Problem

Flux (olafkfreund/nixarchy-flux) links the desktop to Android and iOS phones, tablets and Macs, but Nixi knows nothing
about those devices. What Flux shows today are desktop popups, and Nixi already receives them through `Inbox`
(`app/server.py:3032`): a copied phone notification as "App · Phone" with the phone's own buttons
(`nixarchy-flux internal/core/notifications.go:327`), and "Call from X" (`telephony.go:172`). Showing those again
would add nothing.

What is missing is what a popup can't do:

- **The phone is somewhere else.** With the phone across the room or in a bag, finding it means opening Flux's window
  or a terminal. Its battery runs out unnoticed.
- **Sending something to the phone takes clicks.** A link, the clipboard or a screenshot goes through Flux's window or
  CLI, and nobody tells you when it has arrived.
- **A call is not a meeting to Nixi.** While the phone rings or a call is on, Nixi can keep talking over it. After a
  missed call, answering takes the phone.
- **Texts need the phone.** Reading a text or answering one means picking the phone up, even when it is connected.
- **A wait on the phone is easy to miss.** When `sudo` or an SSH sign waits for the phone's fingerprint, the only hint
  is a dim line in a terminal that may be hidden.

A normal Nixi plugin can't fill these gaps. The sandbox (`app/plugins.py:574-668`) has no runtime directory and no
localhost, a run lasts at most 8 s and returns only a board, and a plugin can't speak on its own or show buttons. The
Flux socket can't be passed to a plugin either: any client of `fluxd.sock` can add and run any shell command, change
settings, send any file and send texts (`internal/core/api.go:257-491`, `docs/security.md:145`).

## Proposed outcome

With Flux running and the new Phone switch on in Settings:

1. **Find my phone.** "Where's my phone?" rings it. With several devices connected, a card shows one button per
   device. A device that is offline, or can't ring (an iPhone), gets a plain answer.
2. **Send to phone or tablet.** "Send this to my phone" sends a link on the clipboard as a link, other clipboard text
   as text, and "this screenshot" as a file. Nixi says when it has arrived or failed. A file that arrives from the
   phone gets a card with Open and Copy.
3. **Phone status and battery.** "How's my phone?" shows a board with battery, charging and connection per device. A
   connected phone below 15% and not charging gets one nudge at a good moment, and only again after it has charged.
   Plugin boards can read a clean phone status (name, connection, battery) that never holds message text.
4. **Calls.** While the phone rings or a call is on, Nixi is quiet as in a meeting. When it rings he says one line,
   "Anna is calling on your Pixel", never during a meeting or Do Not Disturb, and the copied call popup is not shown a
   second time. After a missed call a card offers Text back with an editable message.
5. **Messages.** "Read my texts" shows the newest threads on a card and reads them aloud. "Reply to Anna, I'm on my way"
   makes a draft card. Nothing is sent until the Send button is clicked, and Nixi says whether it went or failed.
6. **Fingerprint waiting.** While sudo or an SSH sign waits on the phone, a card says "Touch the fingerprint sensor on
   your Pixel (sudo)", and it closes when the wait ends.

Without Flux, or with the switch off, nothing changes.

## Affected users and systems

- Nixi (`nixoarchy-snowflake`): `app/server.py`, a new phone module, `app/static/index.html`, `app/lang/*.json`,
  tests. The issue and these artifacts live in `nixi-nixarchy`, as for #227.
- Flux (`nixarchy-flux`): the snapshot additions for call status and fingerprint waiting, under
  olafkfreund/nixarchy-flux#57. Outcomes 4 and 6 need them; 1, 2, 3 and 5 don't.
- The owner's desktop and paired phones and tablets. iOS sends no texts or calls (`nixarchy-flux docs/ios.md`).

## Constraints

- **Built into Nixi, not a sandboxed plugin** (owner decision, 2026-11-13). The plugin sandbox and its tests don't
  change. The only addition plugins see is the clean phone status.
- **Nixi uses a fixed list of Flux calls**: ring, share link/text/files, clipboard send, open/copy a transfer, read and
  send texts, reply to a notification. Never commands, settings, automation, pairing, updates or streams. The agent and
  plugins can't reach that list; only Nixi's own phrase handlers and clicks can.
- **Sending a text or a reply takes a click**, as Mail sending does (`mail_send`, `CLICK_KEY`).
- **Message text stays out** of agent prompts, logs and caches, as with Mail. One-time codes and secrets are removed
  before speech or display (`mail_scrub`, `Inbox.OTP`).
- **ElevenLabs reads everything, message text included** (owner decision, 2026-11-13; the voice stays ElevenLabs, see
  memory "Nixi stays on ElevenLabs").
- **Off by default.** When Flux is missing, the Phone switch is off, or Flux stops, Nixi behaves as today.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - herdr agents through Flux: Nixi watches herdr itself.
  - Desktop media and Do Not Disturb: Nixi has them.
  - Phone webcam, mic and speaker: they would fight Nixi's own audio.
  - Touchpad, screen mirror, remote desktop, motion, pairing, updates: Flux's own windows stay the place for them.
- Every new line of text is in `app/lang/{en,nb,pl}.json` (`tests/test_lang.py`).
- Tests never touch the live HOME, runtime dir, voice or window (`tests/nixi_env.py`, issue #176).

## Open questions

- Does Flux's device view carry a "last seen" time? If not, an offline device is just "not connected".
- Does Nixi speak copied notifications aloud through `Inbox` today? That decides where a call is de-duplicated.
- Should "send this to my phone" also cover the focused browser tab's URL, or only the clipboard and a screenshot?
