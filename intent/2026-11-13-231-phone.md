# Intent: the phone in Nixi, through Flux

## Problem

Flux (olafkfreund/nixarchy-flux) links the desktop to Android and iOS phones, tablets and Macs. Flux can already ring
a phone, send links and files, read and send texts, and it posts desktop popups:
- a copied phone notification as "App · Phone" with the phone's buttons (`nixarchy-flux internal/core/notifications.go:328-330`)
- "Call from X" and "Missed call from X" (`telephony.go:172-182`)
- "Received <file>" with Open and Show in folder (`share.go:513-516`)

Nixi shows those popups through `Inbox` (`app/server.py:3032`). It does so only when proactive mode and notification
takeover are on, the companion is awake and its window connected, and no app is fullscreen (`server.py:3110`). For
non-mail popups it speaks only the category, never the text (`app/static/index.html:2975-2977`, `3045-3046`).

What is missing is Nixi's access to the phone, where a talking companion helps and a popup doesn't:

- **The phone is somewhere else.** Finding it means opening Flux's window or a terminal. Its battery runs out
  unnoticed.
- **Sending something to the phone takes clicks**, through Flux's window or CLI.
- **A call is not a meeting to Nixi.** While the phone rings or a call is on, Nixi can keep talking. Worse, his
  follow-up listening after he speaks can catch the phone conversation. After a missed call, answering takes the
  phone.
- **Texts take the phone or Flux's window.** Nixi can't read one or answer one.
- **The phone stays loud in a meeting Nixi knows about.** Meeting Mode turns on the desktop's Do Not Disturb, and
  Flux's Sync Do Not Disturb passes it to an Android phone (`nixarchy-flux docs/features.md:500-514`). Nixi also counts
  a calendar item a minute away as a meeting (`server.py:2207-2208`), and that sets no Do Not Disturb, so the phone
  rings through it. Nixi can't tell whether the phone is silent.
- **A wait on the phone is easy to miss.** While `sudo` (through `flux-approve`) or an SSH sign waits for approval on
  the phone, the hint is a terminal line that may be hidden. `ssh` prints nothing at all (`nixarchy-flux docs/ssh.md`).

A normal Nixi plugin can't fill these gaps:
- The sandbox (`app/plugins.py:574-668`) has no runtime directory and no localhost.
- A run lasts at most 8 s and returns only a board.
- A plugin can't speak on its own or show buttons.

Giving a plugin the Flux socket is no fix. Any client of `fluxd.sock` can add and run any shell command, change
settings, send any file and send texts (`nixarchy-flux internal/core/api.go:257-491`).

## Proposed outcome

With Flux running and the new Phone switch on in Settings:

1. **Find my phone.** "Where's my phone?" rings it. With several devices that can ring, a card shows one button per
   device. Phones and iPhones both ring (`nixarchy-flux docs/ios.md:248`). An offline device gets "Pixel isn't
   connected, last seen 20 minutes ago", using the device's `lastSeen` (`internal/core/device.go:274`).
2. **Send to phone or tablet.** "Send this to my phone" sends:
   - a link on the clipboard as a link
   - other clipboard text as text
   - "this screenshot" as a file

   For a link or text Nixi says it was sent, since Flux gets no receipt for those (`share.go:907-931`,
   `clipboard.go:560-570`). For a file he follows Flux's transfer state and says done, queued until the phone is back,
   or failed. Files that arrive from the phone keep Flux's own popup; Nixi adds nothing.
3. **Phone status and battery.**
   - "How's my phone?" shows a board with battery, charging and connection per device.
   - A connected phone below 15% and not charging gets one nudge at a good moment, never in a meeting. It comes again
     only after the phone has charged.
   - Plugin boards can ask for a clean phone status: name, connection and battery, never message text.
4. **Calls.**
   - While the phone rings or a call is on, Nixi stops talking and drops queued speech. He closes follow-up listening
     and holds new speech until the call ends.
   - When it rings he says one line, "Anna is calling on your Pixel". He doesn't when the desktop is locked, in a
     meeting, or under Do Not Disturb.
   - Flux's copied call popup and its category speech aren't shown or said a second time.
   - After a missed call with a known number, a card offers Text back with an editable message.
5. **Messages.**
   - "Read my texts" shows the newest threads on a card. Nixi reads them aloud only when the desktop is unlocked and
     no meeting or call is on, and checks again just before playing.
   - "Reply to Anna, I'm on my way" makes a draft card. Nixi says whether the text went, is pending or failed, from
     Flux's outbox.
   - Nothing is sent until the Send button is clicked by hand.
6. **Phone silent in meetings.** When Nixi senses a meeting and the Android phone's Do Not Disturb isn't already on, he
   turns it on through Flux. He turns it off afterwards only if he was the one who turned it on, so a phone you
   silenced yourself stays silent. Meetings already covered by Meeting Mode and Sync Do Not Disturb keep working as they
   do.
7. **Approve on the phone.** While sudo or an SSH sign waits on the phone, a card says "Approve on your Pixel (sudo)".
   It closes when the wait ends, however it ends.

Without Flux, with the switch off, or with an older Flux that lacks the new fields, nothing changes. Outcomes 4, 6 and 7
then stay off, and the Settings line says so.

## Affected users and systems

- Nixi (`nixoarchy-snowflake`): `app/server.py`, a new phone module, `app/stages.py` (board history),
  `app/plugins.py` (the new phone-status input), `app/static/index.html`, `app/lang/*.json`, and tests. The issue and
  these artifacts live in `nixi-nixarchy`, as for #227.
- Flux (`nixarchy-flux`): snapshot additions under olafkfreund/nixarchy-flux#57. Outcomes 4, 6 and 7 need them; 1, 2,
  3 and 5 don't.
- The owner's desktop and paired devices. iOS sends no texts, calls or copied notifications, and a backgrounded iPhone
  goes offline (`nixarchy-flux docs/ios.md:297-304`). Nixi offers only what the selected device can do.

## Constraints

- **Built into Nixi, not a sandboxed plugin** (owner decision, 2026-11-13). The sandbox's isolation doesn't change.
  Plugins gain one read-only input, the clean phone status, with its tests. The sandbox tests are run with
  `NIXI_SANDBOX_TESTS=require` (README "Working on it").
- **Nixi uses a fixed list of Flux calls**: ring, share a link, text or files, send the clipboard, read texts, send a
  text, reply to a notification, and the Do Not Disturb change in outcome 6. Never commands, settings, automation,
  pairing, updates or streams. The list is Nixi's own rule; Flux's socket is not scoped. The agent and plugins can't
  reach the list. The agent can't send texts through `flux-cli` or the socket either; that is guarded like `gog` sends
  (#219, `click_only`).
- **Sending is click-only, checked by the server.** A send needs the click key (`click_ok`, `server.py:80`), as
  approval answers do (`server.py:8188`). A token in the page isn't enough. Mail's send doesn't check it today, so it
  isn't the model here.
- **Phone content stays private**, like Mail and more:
  - Message text, caller names and numbers stay out of agent prompts, logs, caches and memory.
  - Screen capture is refused while a phone board or draft is on screen, like `mail_on_screen` (`server.py:6805-6810`).
  - Phone boards are kept out of Back history, as mail is (`app/stages.py:428-430`), and cleared when Phone is
    switched off (cf. `mail_reset`, `server.py:6794-6803`).
  - Removing one-time codes and secrets (`mail_scrub`, `Inbox.OTP`) runs before speech and display. It is best effort.
- **ElevenLabs reads everything, message text and caller names included** (owner decision, 2026-11-13). Only scrubbed
  text reaches it, and it is gated as above.
- **Off by default.**
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - herdr agents through Flux: Nixi watches herdr itself.
  - Desktop media and the desktop's Do Not Disturb: Nixi has them.
  - Phone webcam, mic and speaker: they would fight Nixi's own audio.
  - Touchpad, screen mirror, remote desktop, motion, pairing, updates: these stay in Flux's own windows.
  - A second popup for received files.
- Every new line of text is in `app/lang/{en,nb,pl}.json` (`tests/test_lang.py`).
- Tests never touch the live HOME, runtime dir, voice or window (`tests/nixi_env.py`, #176). `fluxd` isn't installed
  on this desktop, so live checks need a host that has it.

## Open questions

- Should "send this to my phone" also cover the focused browser tab's URL, or only the clipboard and a screenshot?
- Outcome 6 needs Flux to report the phone's Do Not Disturb and let Nixi change it (#57). Should a calendar-only meeting
  silence the phone at all, or only Meeting Mode's own meetings? The current sync already covers those.
- Outgoing calls also report "talking". Nixi would go quiet for them too, which seems right; confirm.
