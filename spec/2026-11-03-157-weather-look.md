# Spec: Nixi dresses for the weather, and it looks good

Code: `nixoarchy-snowflake` (the companion). Line numbers are at `main`
`2e36123`. Slice 1 changes only `app/static/index.html`. Slice 2 changes the
page and `app/server.py`.

The approved intent's answers:
- Open-Meteo for the current place, falling back to `omarchy weather`;
- a scarf at "feels like" ≤ 5 °C and a beanie at ≤ −5 °C, with a 2 °C
  margin;
- a poll every 30 min, plus on a place change;
- this branch covers slices 1 and 2.

## What's there today (checked)

- **Layers.**
  - `#guy` (`viewBox -150 -150 300 300`, `:586`) holds `#cam` (`:602`).
    `#cam` gets the punch-zoom and shake (`:1364-1367`), and holds the
    halo, rays, `#fxBack`, the shadow and `#body` (`:612`).
  - `frame()` moves, squashes and rolls `#body` every frame.
  - Inside `#body`, from back to front: `#accBack`, `#sitBack`, the ball
    (the plate, then six lambdas reaching out to radius about 125,
    `:1036-1039`), the face, the gloss, `#accFront`, `#sitFront`,
    `#frost`.
- **The top lambda** covers y −125 to −70. Anything in `#sitBack` above his
  head is hidden behind it. The party hat's pom-pom, at about y −122, is
  the highest thing that fits the window today.
- **The umbrella** (`drawSit`, `:940-944`):
  - one group in `#sitFront`, scaled to 0.75;
  - a flat `#f2c94c` canopy, a white J-handle ending in mid-air at
    (0, −58), and four drops in the same scaled group.
  - `frame()` places it with no rotation (`put(SIT_P.um,um,0,-84)`,
    `:1511`) and moves the drops by a vertical translate with no fade
    (`:1512`).
  - Everything there runs inside `if(!(off&&sitSettled))` (`:1509-1510`).
- **The top-zone choice** (`:1504`): party hat > umbrella > nightcap, only
  with `LOOK.skin==='none'`.
- **`sitStep`** (`:1909`):
  - a look goes on after 3 s of signal and off after 8 s without it;
  - each key maps to `info['look_'+key]`, except `rain`, which maps to
    `look_weather` and `SIT.weather==='rain'`.
- **Sway:** `w` (`:1502`) is the hanging-sway angle, which the nightcap and
  party hat use. `G.tilt` is his body tilt spring (`:1204`, `:1333`).
- **Reduced motion:** `REDUCED` is defined at `:1973` and used only by the
  answer cards.
- **Server:**
  - **The fetch:** `Ambient.fetch` (`server.py:1275-1281`) runs `omarchy
    weather status` and `icon` every 1800 s.
    - `data["weather"]` becomes `cold` (< 0 °C), `hot` (> 28 °C) or
      `None`, for the frost cue.
    - `sky` becomes `rain`, `snow` or `None`.
  - **The gate:** `Situation` (`:1377`) sends `weather = sky` only with
    `proactive`, `look_weather` and `ambient_weather` all on.
  - **Places:** `location()` (`:1085`) returns `now`, `home` and `work`. A
    place has `latitude` and `longitude` only when it was picked from a
    suggestion.
  - **No weather refresh on a place change:** `location_act` (`:1162`)
    clears `LOC_CACHE` but doesn't refresh the weather.
  - **Settings refresh only for `ambient_*` keys:** `/api/settings` pokes
    `Ambient` only when an `ambient_*` key changes (`:4884`).
  - **`/api/state`** shows `Ambient.snapshot()`, but not the situation
    (`:4555`).

## Design

### Slice 1: rain and snow that look right (page only)

#### 1. A held umbrella

- **Where:** the umbrella stays in `#sitFront`, as today. There's no room
  above his head, and from `#sitBack` the top lambda would cover the
  canopy (agy).
- **Size:** larger than today's 81 units, as large as `accessories_fit`
  allows through his biggest moves. The target is about 110 units across.
- **Shape:**
  - a domed canopy in the same yellow, with a darker underside arc
    (`shade(yellow,-.35)`) along the scallops, so it has depth;
  - three thin rib lines;
  - a small ferrule on top.
- **Held:**
  - the canopy tilts about 15° and sits over the top of his head, offset
    to the left;
  - a dark shaft runs down to the right, to the **right foot of his top
    lambda** (about (64, −118));
  - a J-hook curls round that foot, so it reads as held.
  - The plan pins the exact points from a GPU-off preview.
- **Sway:**
  - the whole umbrella rotates about the grip point by `w*.45`, clamped to
    ±6°;
  - the canopy is no more than about 75 units from the grip, so a 6° rock
    moves it by at most about 8 units;
  - in slice 2, the wind tilt is added through a spring (§7).
- **The old drops** inside the umbrella group are removed (see §2).

#### 2. Rain and snow as their own layer

- **A new `<g id="wx">`,** a direct child of `#guy`, **before** `#cam`:
  - it doesn't roll, squash, zoom or shake with him (agy);
  - it's behind him, so the ball and eyes cover it and it never covers
    them.
- **Rain:**
  - 8 streaks, 14 units long and 2.4 wide, in `#9fd3ff`;
  - each falls along its own slant, 12° from vertical (22° when windy, in
    slice 2), at about 220 units/s;
  - start positions are staggered between y −150 and +40. Each start x is
    chosen so that the whole fall stays inside x ±145 for the current
    slant (agy: a slanted streak from x ±135 would leave the window);
  - opacity follows `0.85·sin(π·p)`, so each streak fades in and out
    instead of jumping.
- **Snow:**
  - 6 flakes, circles of r 2–3.5, white at 0.55–0.9 opacity;
  - they fall at about 22 units/s, weave sideways by `sin(t·0.9 + i)·6`,
    and stay inside x ±145;
  - no umbrella. The existing excited poke when snow starts stays.
- **Rain and snow never show together:**
  - a new `sitOn.snow` key, with the same 3 s / 8 s rule as the others;
  - when `snow` turns on, `rain` turns off at once, and the other way
    round. A change from rain to snow never shows the umbrella in snow
    (Codex).
- **`sitStep` maps** `rain` and `snow` (and slice 2's `cold1` and `cold2`)
  to `look_weather` and to their own condition.
- **When the layer shows:**
  - rain while `sitOn.rain` is on. It doesn't need the umbrella, so it
    shows even when a skin blocks the top zone;
  - snow while `sitOn.snow` is on.
  - It's driven **outside** the `off&&sitSettled` guard, which only knows
    the body looks.
- **Cost and modes:**
  - the layer writes only while it shows;
  - lowFx (`:1228`) halves the count;
  - CSS hides `#wx` in `body.bar`, `body.hover`, `body.calm` and
    `body.goohide` (the notch melt).

#### 3. Reduced motion, for every weather item

- `REDUCED` moves up to where `frame()` can read it.
- Under reduced motion:
  - particles stand still: 3 streaks or 3 flakes, at fixed positions;
  - the umbrella, scarf and beanie get no sway or wind tilt;
  - his body gets no wind lean and no shiver;
  - items still fade in and out.

#### 4. Settings text (`:713`)

"Dress for the weather" reads: "An umbrella he holds in the rain, falling
snow, and (with a place set) a scarf and beanie when it's cold."

### Slice 2: dressed for the cold, moved by the wind

#### 5. Weather data (server)

- **The place:** `location()`'s `now` if it has coordinates, else `home` if
  it does. Its key is the coordinates rounded to 2 decimals.
- **The gate:** only with `proactive`, `ambient_weather` and
  `look_weather` all on.
  - The `omarchy weather` call keeps its current gate (`ambient_weather`),
    so the frost cue is unchanged.
- **The request,** with a place: one GET with `urllib`, an 8 s timeout and
  no key:
  `https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast?latitude=…&longitude=…&current=temperature_2m,apparent_temperature,weather_code,wind_speed_10m,wind_direction_10m&timezone=auto`.
- **The reading** it gives, field by field:
  - **`sky`:** codes 51–67, 80–82 and 95–99 → `rain`; 71–77 and 85–86 →
    `snow`; anything else, or a missing code → `None`.
  - **`heavy`:** codes 65, 67 and 82.
  - **`feel`:** `apparent_temperature`. **`temp`:** `temperature_2m`.
  - **`wind`:** `wind_speed_10m` in km/h. **`dir`:** `wind_direction_10m`,
    where it blows *from*.
  - **Validation:** each field must be a number in range (temperatures
    −80…60, wind 0…400, direction 0…360), or it becomes `None`.
- **Precedence**, one rule (both reviews found the old wording
  contradicted itself):
  1. A good Open-Meteo reading for the **current place key**, less than
     2 h old, is used: for `sky`, `feel`, `wind`, `dir` and `heavy`, and
     its `temp` for the frost cue.
  2. Otherwise, the `omarchy weather` reading is used:
     - `sky` from its icon;
     - `feel` = `temp` from its status;
     - `wind` from `Wind ↙17km/h` (the number);
     - `dir = None`, `heavy = false`.
  3. If neither exists, everything is `None`. He wears nothing for the
     weather.
  - A failed request doesn't overwrite a good reading. A reading for a
    different place key is never used.
- **Refreshes:**
  - on a place change made in Settings (`location_act` pokes `Ambient`);
  - on a place change noticed by Wi-Fi: `Situation` compares the place key
    on each tick (`location()` is cached for 60 s) and pokes `Ambient` when
    it changes;
  - when `proactive` or `look_weather` changes in `/api/settings`, as
    well as `ambient_*` (agy).
  - The interval stays 1800 s.
- **`/api/state`** gains `weather`: `{sky, cold, windy, lean, heavy, feel,
  wind, source}`, so the live check can see it.

#### 6. What the page gets

- **The `situation` event gains:** `cold` (0, 1 or 2), `windy` (boolean),
  `lean` (−1, 0 or +1) and `heavy` (boolean).
- **They're computed in `Situation`, with margins** so they don't flicker:
  - **`cold`:**
    - from 0 to 1 at feel ≤ 5; from 1 to 2 at ≤ −5;
    - from 2 to 1 at > −3; from 1 to 0 at > 7;
    - from 2 straight to 0 at > 7.
  - **`windy`:** on at ≥ 25 km/h, off at < 20.
  - **`lean`:** which way is "into the wind" on screen. East is screen
    right. `s = sin(radians(dir))`; `lean = +1` if s > 0.3, −1 if s < −0.3,
    else 0. A north or south wind, or an unknown direction, leans no way,
    but `windy` still slants the rain (agy).
  - The margin state resets when the place key changes and when the gate
    turns off, so a new place starts fresh.
- **The same gate** as `weather` (all three switches).
- **The weather quips** (`Nudge`, `:1644`) read `weather` as today. No new
  quips.

#### 7. The outfit (page)

- **Scarf** (`sitFront`):
  - a knitted band, with stripes in the accent colour and white, across
    the lower part of his ball, just above where the tie hangs;
  - one tail on the right;
  - on at `cold ≥ 1`;
  - the meeting tie wins, and so does a plugin prop anchored at `base`.
- **Beanie** (top zone):
  - a ribbed cuff and a pom-pom, in the accent colour;
  - on at `cold = 2`;
  - the order becomes party hat > umbrella > beanie > nightcap, only with
    no skin.
- **Both** use the 3 s / 8 s rule (`sitOn.cold1`, `sitOn.cold2`), and must
  pass `accessories_fit`.
- **Shiver:**
  - at `cold ≥ 1`, while idle, about every 25 s;
  - a 0.6 s sideways jitter of ±1.5 units on `#body`.
- **Wind,** through a new spring `G.wind`, so nothing snaps:
  - its target is `lean` while windy, else 0;
  - his tilt target leans `4°·G.wind`;
  - hat tops, the umbrella's canopy and the scarf tail all move
    **downwind**. Top items and hanging items rotate with opposite signs
    to do that (agy); the plan fixes the signs with the preview;
  - rain slants downwind: 22° while windy, flipping with `lean`. With
    `lean` 0 it slants 22° the default way.
- **Heavy rain:** 12 streaks at 1.4× speed (6 under lowFx).
- **No day/night change.** Night stays on the existing clock signal. The
  forecast's day/night is left for the sky marks (later). This corrects
  the intent's slice 2 list, which named day/night as data that isn't
  needed yet (Codex).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Parsing more of `omarchy weather`'s text.** It has no "feels like"
  temperature and no weather code, and it depends on Omarchy's wording and
  icon glyphs. It stays only as the fallback.
- **The canopy in `#sitBack`, with only the hook in front.** The top lambda
  would cover the canopy.
- **CSS animations for the particles.** They'd run while he's hidden and
  bypass lowFx.
- **Particles inside `#body` or `#cam`.** They'd roll, squash, zoom and
  shake with him.
- **A raincoat or hood, fog, and a cloud over his head.** They hide his
  shape, or need blur that's unsafe on this GPU.
- **Polling every 15 min.** Clothes don't need it, and place changes
  already refresh.

## Risks

- **A bigger umbrella clips at the window top in his biggest bounce.** The
  plan sizes it with `accessories_fit`.
- **The held umbrella may look awkward at first.** The plan pins its points
  from previews before the code is final.
- **Open-Meteo is down.** After 2 h without a good reading he falls back to
  `omarchy weather`. Rain and snow still work, and the scarf and beanie
  follow the actual temperature instead of "feels like". Heavy rain and
  wind lean wait for the forecast to come back.
- **Privacy:** the coordinates go to Open-Meteo only with all three
  switches on and a place that has coordinates.
- **CPU:** at most 12 particles plus the outfit, transform and opacity
  only, and they write only while showing.
- **Hosts:** p620 and razer.

## Verification

  - new:
    - snow;
    - scarf and beanie;
    - umbrella, beanie and scarf with `G.wind` at ±1;

  - `#wx` is a child of `#guy` and not inside `#cam`;
  - streak opacity is below 0.2 at the start and end of a fall;
  - a change from rain to snow never shows the umbrella and snow together;
  - under reduced motion (emulated through CDP), particle and body
    transforms don't change between frames;
  - `#wx` is hidden in bar mode.
- **Server unit tests** (`tests/test_weather.py`), with a fake `urlopen`
  and a fake `location()`:

  - a switch off → no Open-Meteo call;

- **Previews** in headless Chromium with the GPU off: rain, heavy rain,

## Review (Codex and agy, read-only)

Confirmed in the code, and fixed above:

- The rain layer has to sit outside `#cam`, not just outside `#body`, or it
  zooms and shakes with him (both).
- With the canopy in `#sitBack`, the top lambda would cover it. The
  umbrella stays in front, with a short lever so its sway stays in the
  window (agy).
- Rain and snow could overlap for 5 s (Codex).
- The failure rules contradicted each other. Now there's one precedence
  rule, keyed by place (both).
- The wind maths: degrees vs radians, north and south winds read as calm,
  and opposite signs for hanging and standing items (agy).
- Slanted rain left the window (agy).
- Toggling `proactive` or `look_weather` didn't refresh (agy); a Wi-Fi
  place change didn't either (Codex).
- `/api/state` didn't carry the new fields (both).
- Reduced motion missed the body lean, and the wind tilt would snap (agy).
- The new keys need mapping in `sitStep`, and the particles must run
  outside the `sitSettled` guard (agy).
- Missing slot tests (agy).
- Day/night isn't needed yet (Codex).
