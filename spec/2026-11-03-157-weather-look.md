# Spec: Nixi dresses for the weather, and it looks good

Code: `nixoarchy-snowflake` (the companion). Line numbers are at `main`
`2e36123`. Slice 1 changes only `app/static/index.html`. Slice 2 changes the
page and `app/server.py`. The approved intent's answers: Open-Meteo for the
current place, falling back to `omarchy weather`; scarf at "feels like" ≤ 5 °C
and beanie at ≤ −5 °C with a 2 °C margin; poll every 30 min plus on a place
change; this branch is slices 1 and 2.

## What's there today (checked)

- **Layers.**
  - `#guy` (`viewBox -150 -150 300 300`, `:586`) holds `#body` (`:612`).
    `frame()` moves, squashes and rolls `#body` every frame.
  - Inside `#body`, from back to front: `#accBack`, `#sitBack`, the ball,
    the face, the gloss, `#accFront`, `#sitFront`, `#frost`.
- **The umbrella** (`drawSit`, `:940-944`):
  - one group in `#sitFront`, scaled to 0.75;
  - a flat `#f2c94c` canopy, a white J-handle ending at (0, −58), and four
    drops inside the same scaled group.
  - `frame()` places it with no rotation (`put(SIT_P.um,um,0,-84)`,
    `:1511`) and moves the drops by a vertical translate with no fade
    (`:1512`).
- **The top-zone choice** (`:1504`): party hat > umbrella > nightcap, only
  with `LOOK.skin==='none'`.
- **Looks come and go slowly:** `sitStep` (`:1909`) turns a look on after
  3 s of signal and off after 8 s without it. `rain` is the only weather
  look, and comes from `SIT.weather==='rain'`.
- **Sway:** `w` (`:1502`) is the shared hanging-sway angle. The nightcap
  and party hat use it; the umbrella doesn't.
- **Reduced motion:** `REDUCED` is defined at `:1973` and used only by the
  answer cards.
- **Server:**
  - `Ambient.fetch` (`server.py:1275-1281`) runs `omarchy weather status`
    and `icon` every 1800 s.
    - `data["weather"]` becomes `cold` (< 0 °C), `hot` (> 28 °C) or
      `None`, for the frost cue.
    - `sky` becomes `rain`, `snow` or `None`.
  - `Situation` (`:1377`) sends `weather = sky` only with `proactive`,
    `look_weather` and `ambient_weather` all on.
  - `location()` (`:1085`) returns `now`, `home` and `work`. A place has
    `latitude` and `longitude` only when it was picked from a suggestion.
  - `location_act` (`:1162`) clears `LOC_CACHE`, but doesn't refresh the
    weather.

## Design

### Slice 1: rain and snow that look right (page only)

#### 1. A held umbrella

- **Size:** a canopy about 124 units wide (±62), up from 81, and as large
  as `accessories_fit` allows through his biggest moves. Its top sits about
  10 units lower than the party hat's pom-pom, the tallest thing that fits
  today.
- **Shape:**
  - a domed canopy in the same yellow, with a darker underside arc
    (`shade(yellow,-.35)`) under the scallops, so it has depth;
  - three thin rib lines from the ferrule to the scallop points;
  - a small ferrule on top.
- **Held by a lambda tip:**
  - the shaft runs from under the canopy's centre down to his
    **upper-right lambda tip**;
  - a J-hook curls round that tip;
  - the plan pins the tip's coordinates from the ball geometry.
- **Depth:**
  - the canopy and shaft go in a new group in `#sitBack`, so the shaft
    passes behind his ball;
  - only the hook goes in `#sitFront`, so it's in front of the tip and
    reads as a grip;
  - both are placed with the same `put()` call.
- **Sway:** it rotates about the grip point by `w*.45`, plus the wind tilt
  in slice 2. The canopy then rocks while the grip stays on the tip.
- **The old drops** inside the umbrella group are removed (see §2).

#### 2. Rain and snow as their own layer

- **A new `<g id="wx">`** in `#guy`, **before** `#body`:
  - It's outside `#body`, so it never rolls, squashes or bounces with him.
  - It's behind him, so his ball and eyes are never covered, and the
    canopy hides the streaks that pass behind it.
- **Rain:**
  - 8 streaks, 14 units long and 2.4 wide, in `#9fd3ff`, at 12° from
    vertical;
  - each falls along its own angle (the translate follows the slant);
  - fall speed about 220 units/s, with staggered start x and phase across
    x ±135 and y −150 to +40;
  - opacity follows `0.85·sin(π·p)`, so each streak fades in and out
    instead of jumping.
- **Snow:**
  - 6 flakes, circles of r 2–3.5, white at 0.55–0.9 opacity;
  - they fall at about 22 units/s and weave sideways by `sin(t·0.9 + i)·6`;
  - no umbrella. The existing excited poke when snow starts stays.
- **When the layer shows:**
  - rain while the rain look is on; it doesn't need the umbrella, so it
    shows even when a skin blocks the top zone;
  - snow while a new `snow` look is on. That's a `sitOn.snow` entry, with
    the same 3 s / 8 s rule, from `SIT.weather==='snow'` and
    `look_weather`.
- **Cost and modes:**
  - the layer writes only while it shows, like the props (`:1513`);
  - lowFx (`:1228`) halves the count;
  - CSS hides `#wx` in `body.bar`, `body.hover` and `body.calm`.

#### 3. Reduced motion, for every weather item

- `REDUCED` moves up to where `frame()` can read it.
- Under reduced motion:
  - particles stand still at fixed positions: 3 streaks or 3 flakes;
  - the umbrella, scarf and beanie get no sway, wind tilt or shiver;
  - they still fade in and out.

#### 4. Settings text (`:713`)

"Dress for the weather" reads: "An umbrella he holds in the rain, falling
snow, and (with a place set) a scarf and beanie when it's cold."

### Slice 2: dressed for the cold, moved by the wind

#### 5. Weather data (server)

- **Where:** `Ambient.fetch`'s weather branch (`server.py:1275`).
- **The place:** `location()`'s `now` if it has coordinates, else `home`
  if it does.
- **With a place:** one GET with `urllib`, an 8 s timeout and no key:
  `https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast?latitude=…&longitude=…&current=temperature_2m,apparent_temperature,weather_code,wind_speed_10m,wind_direction_10m&timezone=auto`.
  - `sky`: codes 51–67 and 80–82 → `rain`; 71–77 and 85–86 → `snow`; 95–99
    → `rain`; otherwise `None`.
  - `heavy`: codes 65, 67 and 82.
  - `feel`: `apparent_temperature`.
  - `wind`: `wind_speed_10m` in km/h, and `dir`: `wind_direction_10m`
    (where it blows *from*).
  - `data["weather"]` (frost or thaw) keeps today's rule, on
    `temperature_2m`.
- **With no place,** or when the request fails: today's `omarchy weather`
  path.
  - `feel` is the parsed temperature (there's no "feels like" there).
  - `wind` is parsed from `Wind ↙17km/h` (the number only). `dir` is `None`.
  - `heavy` is false.
- **Failures:**
  - a failed request keeps the last good reading for up to 2 h, then
    clears it;
  - a value outside its sensible range (feel outside −80…60, wind outside
    0…400) is dropped.
- **No request at all** unless `proactive`, `ambient_weather` and
  `look_weather` are all on. Today the fetch runs on `ambient_weather`
  alone. The forecast request needs all three; the `omarchy weather` path
  keeps its current gate, so the frost cue doesn't change.
- **Refresh on a place change:** `location_act` calls `Ambient.poke()`
  after it clears `LOC_CACHE`.
- **The interval stays 1800 s.**

#### 6. What the page gets

- **The `situation` event gains:**
  - `cold`: 0, 1 (scarf) or 2 (scarf and beanie);
  - `wind`: −1, 0 or 1 (which way to lean, 0 when calm);
  - `heavy`: a boolean.
- **They're computed in `Situation`, with margins** so they don't flicker:
  - `cold` goes up to 1 at feel ≤ 5 and drops back at > 7; up to 2 at
    ≤ −5 and back at > −3;
  - windy at ≥ 25 km/h, calm again at < 20;
  - lean sign: `round(sin(dir))`, i.e. into the wind; when `dir` is
    unknown, +1.
- **The same gate** as `weather` (all three switches).
- **The weather quips** (`Nudge`, `:1644`) read `weather` as today. No new
  quips.

#### 7. The outfit (page)

- **Scarf** (`sitFront`):
  - a knitted band, two-tone stripes in the accent colour and white,
    wrapped across the lower part of his ball, just above where the tie
    hangs;
  - one tail on the right that sways with `w` and the wind;
  - on at `cold ≥ 1`;
  - the meeting tie wins, and so does a plugin prop anchored at `base`.
- **Beanie** (top zone):
  - a ribbed cuff and a pom-pom, in the accent colour;
  - on at `cold = 2`;
  - the order becomes party hat > umbrella > beanie > nightcap, only with
    no skin.
- **Both use the 3 s / 8 s rule** (`sitOn.cold1`, `sitOn.cold2`), and both
  must pass `accessories_fit`.
- **Shiver:**
  - at `cold ≥ 1`, while idle (not talking or working), about every 25 s;
  - a 0.6 s sideways jitter of ±1.5 units on `#body`;
  - none under reduced motion.
- **Wind:**
  - while windy, his tilt target leans 4° towards `wind`;
  - the umbrella, beanie and scarf tail tilt a further 8° the same way;
  - rain streaks slant 22° instead of 12°.
- **Heavy rain:** 12 streaks at 1.4× speed (6 under lowFx).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Parse more of `omarchy weather`'s text.** It has no "feels like"
  temperature and no weather code, and it depends on Omarchy's wording and
  icon glyphs. It stays only as the fallback.
- **CSS animations for the particles.** They'd run while he's hidden and
  bypass lowFx. The `frame()` loop already pauses and already has the
  timing.
- **Particles inside `#body`.** They'd roll and squash with him, the
  problem agy found.
- **A raincoat or hood, fog, and a cloud over his head.** They hide his
  shape, or need blur that's unsafe on this GPU (both reviews).
- **Mittens, sunglasses, thunder and sky marks** stay for later, as the
  intent says.
- **Polling every 15 min.** Clothes don't need it. A place change already
  refreshes.

## Risks

- **A bigger umbrella clips at the window top in his biggest bounce.** The
  plan sizes it with `accessories_fit` and shrinks it as needed.
- **Open-Meteo is down or rate-limited.** We keep the last reading for 2 h,
  then fall back to nothing new; the frost cue keeps working from `omarchy
  weather`.
- **Privacy:** the coordinates go to Open-Meteo only with all three
  switches on and a place that has coordinates.
- **CPU:** at most 12 nodes animate, using transform and opacity only.
  They write only while showing.
- **Hosts:** p620 and razer.

  - rain streaks' opacity is below 0.2 at the start and end of a fall;
  - under reduced motion (emulated through CDP), particle transforms don't
    change between two frames;
  - `#wx` is hidden in bar mode.

  and fake `location()`:

  - a switch off → no Open-Meteo call;

  - out-of-range values are dropped;
