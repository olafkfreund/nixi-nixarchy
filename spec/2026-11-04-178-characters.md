# Spec: choose a different body for Nixi

All code is in olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake. Line numbers come from
`mockups/178/code-map.md` and the reviews (approximate: re-find them by
name). The artwork follows agy's direction, which the user chose
(`mockups/178/agy-characters.html`). It is **refitted around the live
eyes**, as the next section explains.

## Design

### 1. The DOM shape stays as it is; only the contents change

Many CSS rules and readers select **direct children** of `#body`:
- `#body>.ball` and `#body>ellipse`, in the notch, goo, pop-out, bar and
  bar-done rules (CSS 32, 76–89);
- `#body>path.ball` and `#body>.nix` (1200–1201);
- `#body > .ball` in `cardPlace` (2662) and the bubble's `geo()` (3641).

So no wrapper group is added. Every character fills the **same elements**:

| Element (a child of `#body`) | Snowflake (today) | Other characters |
|---|---|---|
| `path.ball` (fill) and `#rim` (`path.ball`, stroke) | the hexagon `d` | the character's silhouette `d` |
| `g.ball.nix` | the six lambdas | the character's art behind the eyes (case, stand, keyboard; shell and antenna; wings, horns and tail) |
| the two gloss `ellipse`s | shown | hidden (`display:none`) |
| **new** `g.ball.bfront`, after `#face`, before `#tears` | empty | art in front of the eyes (screen glare, brows, fang) |

Every part is inside a `.ball` element that's a direct child of `#body`, or
is that element. So the existing notch, goo and pop-out rules hide or
animate **all** of a character, with no selector changes. The bar fit test
checks that.

- `.nix`'s width is still "his reach" for `cardPlace` and the bubble: for a
  new character it measures its whole art, which is the right reach. So
  there's no separate `reach` number.
- The glint (2115) lights `.nix` lambdas; it's Snowflake only.

`setCharacter(id)`:
- sets the two `d`s;
- replaces the children of `.nix` and `.bfront` from fixed, built-in markup
  strings, never from settings or plugins;
- toggles the gloss;
- sets `BODY = CHARACTERS[id]`.

Snowflake's strings are exactly what `snowPath()` and `nixLambdas()` build
today.

### 2. The artwork fits the live eye rig

The live eyes are `EYE = {rx:23.5, ry:40.5, cx:27.5, cy:-4.5, tilt:.157}`
(1202): about 47 × 81 each, tilted. At rest they span x ±4…±51 and
y −45…+36, and up to y −52…+43 with mood pops and the talk swell, plus
gaze. agy's mockup drew smaller, upright eyes (`rx 16 ry 26 cx ±24 cy −6`).
So the artwork is redrawn around the real rig, keeping agy's look:

- **Terminal:** the screen grows to hold the full eye extremes with a margin
  (at least x ±60, y −58…+50). The `>▮` prompt moves to the screen's
  bottom-left corner, below y +46.
- **Robot:** the visor grows to at least the same window (rounded corners
  outside the eye extremes). The LED cheeks move outside x ±56.
- **Devil:** the brows sit above y −56, and the grin and fang below y +46.
  The cheeks move outside x ±56.

Each character declares a `faceBox` (its screen, visor or face window).
**The eye rig is never changed**, and there's no per-character eye offset
(intent: one shared rig).

### 3. Anchors, and where translations go

`BODY.anchors`, as `[x, y]` offsets from Snowflake's, so Snowflake's are
all `[0,0]`:

| Anchor | What uses it |
|---|---|
| `head` | skins (halo, crown, sprout, bow); hats (nightcap, party hat, beanie); `PROP_BOX.head`; the weather slot |
| `face` | glasses, shades, headphones (always `[0,0]`, since the eyes don't move) |
| `neck` | scarf, tie |
| `base` | `PROP_BOX.base`, the shadow |
| `left`, `right` | `PROP_BOX.left` and `.right`; the chat and feed props |

**Where the offset is applied:** the frame overwrites `transform` every
frame on each `SIT_P` group (`put()` and `lift()`, 1785–1790), on
`SP2.wob` (the sprout, 1776) and on `SP2.halo` (1778). So the offset goes
on a **new wrapper `<g>` outside** each of those groups, created once in
`drawSit` and `drawSkin`. The frame keeps setting the inner group's
transform exactly as today. `PROP_BOX` entries are shifted by their
anchor's offset when a plugin prop is drawn.

**`fits`:** each character lists the skins and sit props it can wear.
Anything else is hidden on it: frost and the glint are Snowflake only, and
a prop that collides with a body's shape (for example the sprout on the
robot's antenna) is left out of `fits`.

**Still unchanged:** the squash pivot becomes `BODY.pivot` (Snowflake 76),
and the shadow width becomes `BODY.shadow` (Snowflake 62).

### 4. Each character's one moving part

- CSS `@keyframes` (`cursor-blink`, `antenna-pulse`, `tail-swish`) on one
  element per character. Only `transform` and `opacity`, never `filter`.
- **Paused while he's still:** `body.still` is set and cleared inside the
  existing 500 ms `idleCheck()` (3531–3556), not per frame. He's still when
  all of these hold:
  - `performance.now() - Math.max(lastActive, lastUser) > 60000`;
  - not `idleActive()`;
  - not `busy`.

  `lastActive` (976, updated by `wake()` and `poke()` on voice, keys, mouse,
  messages and notifications) and `lastUser` are the page's real activity
  clocks, so he's never "still" at startup or right after voice use. The
  class toggles only when the value changes (it's a `classList.toggle(…,
  bool)`). `renderCue()`'s body-class observer (2055) therefore runs at most
  once per change.
- **Under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`** (the same query as
  `REDUCED`, 1484): no animation.

### 5. Colours

- **Recoloured with the user's body colour** (`--body0..2`, through a
  per-character gradient like `#ballG`): the terminal's case and stand, the
  robot's shell, the devil's body and wings. That's the same role the
  hexagon has today.
- **Fixed:**
  - the terminal's screen (dark phosphor) and its green prompt;
  - the robot's visor (dark) and its blue antenna light;
  - the devil's gold horns and its fang.
- **Contrast.** The eyes sit on the dark screen or visor (terminal, robot),
  or on the body colour (devil, as Snowflake's eyes do today), so eye
  contrast is unchanged in kind. Each fixed accent carries its own dark
  outline (`--rim`, like the hexagon's), so it stays separate from any body
  colour. Contrast is checked between each accent and its own outline or
  background, which is fixed, so it holds for every six-digit body colour
  `look()` accepts.

### 6. The setting

- **`server.py` `look()`** (266–280) accepts `character` in
  `{"snowflake","terminal","robot","devil"}` (default `"snowflake"`). Other
  values are dropped, as unknown keys are today.
- **`state_info`** (5206) sends it.
- **Settings → Look:**
  - a "Character" row of four previews, before Skin;
  - picking one calls `setCharacter` live and saves it;
  - a one-line hint suggests a voice and changes nothing (intent Q3).
- **`miniNixi()`** (1162–1175) draws from the same `CHARACTERS` entry and the
  live `EYE`, which also fixes its eye `cx` of 25.5 against the live 27.5.
- **The Settings row waits for #175 and #176 to merge;** everything else
  can go first.

### 7. Plugin characters: not in this issue

Intent Q1: built-in now. The registry is the extension point. A later issue
adds a plugin `body` slot drawn as a sanitised image (`plugins.py`
388–490), never as markup.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Wrapper groups `#bodyBack` and `#bodyFront`** (the first draft): they
  break every `#body>` selector and reader, and would leave body art showing
  in the notch.
- **Moving or resizing the eyes per character:** against the intent's one
  shared rig, and every mood, gaze and swell would need re-tuning.
- **Replacing every coordinate literal with constants:** dozens of edits.
  One wrapper translate per prop group does the same with a checkable diff,
  and leaves Snowflake's numbers alone.
- **Separate SVG files per character:** fetches, loading states and a new
  trust path, for a few hundred bytes of fixed markup each.
- **Plugin characters now:** the intent deferred them.

## Risks

- **Snowflake drifting from today.** Caught by the pixel comparison below.
- **The eyes colliding with refitted art** at mood extremes: caught by the
  face-box test below.
- **Props off the 340 × 460 surface on a new body:** caught by the fit tests
  below, run per character.
- **Merge conflicts with #175, #176 and #177 in `index.html`:** only the
  Settings row touches what they change, and it waits for their merge.
- **Performance:** CSS transform and opacity only. `still` is computed in
  the 500 ms check, and the slow-frame watcher still applies.

## Verification

- **Snowflake pixel comparison** (`tests/character_fit.mjs` plus
  `test_character_fit.py`, on the existing CDP harness):
  - capture the same states on `origin/main` (a temporary worktree) and on
    the branch: the same Chromium; `prefers-reduced-motion` emulated;
    animation and time frozen (CDP `Animation.setPlaybackRate 0` and a fixed
    `performance.now` offset); a fixed mood, theme and colours;
  - three moods, plus the bar (notch) state;
  - **pass:** no pixel differs by more than 8/255 in any channel, apart from
    at most 0.05 % of pixels (anti-aliasing).
- **For each character:**
  - **the face box:** for idle, happy, surprised, the full talk swell and
    gaze at its 4 extremes, both eyes' boxes are inside `faceBox`. They
    don't intersect any `.avoid`-marked art (brows, mouth, fang, prompt,
    cheeks);
  - **the whole surface:** the existing `accessories_fit.mjs` and the sit
    cases (hats, scarf, tie, headphones, glasses, chat and feed, weather)
    run for every skin and prop it `fits`. Everything stays within
    340 × 460 through a bounce. Props not in `fits` aren't rendered;
  - **the bar state:** nothing of the character is visible in the notch
    (the computed opacity of every `#body` child with `.ball` is 0, as
    today);
  - **the touch:** it runs. It's paused under `body.still` and under reduced
    motion, and `body.still` isn't set at startup or 1 s after `wake()`.
- **Unit test:** `look()` accepts the four characters, drops anything else,
  and defaults to snowflake.
- **Contrast:** each fixed accent against its own outline or background is
  at least 3:1, computed from the CSS values.
- **Full suite and the build.**
- **Live on p620,** after a deploy the user approves:
  - each character, picked in Settings → Look;
  - blink, talk, listen;
  - a hat and the scarf;
  - the touch pauses when he's still;
  - Snowflake as before.
