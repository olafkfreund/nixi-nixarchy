# Spec: Nixi shrinks when idle and pops up when something happens

All code is in `olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake`, on one branch from `main`
**after #138's PR B is merged**, because both change the same parts of
`index.html`. Line numbers below are from `main` at `51ad40a`.

## Decisions on the intent's open questions

The intent was approved without changes, so the proposals apply.

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Default | `idle_mode: "shrink"` (on). The options are `"off"`, `"shrink"` and `"doze"`; doze also shrinks first. |
| 2 | Timings | `idle_shrink_s: 45`, `idle_doze_s: 300`. After an event he holds full size for 12 s, or 20 s for "needs you" and prompts. These are fixed constants, not settings. |
| 3 | Peek size | 40 %, tucked toward the screen corner nearest to him. |
| 4 | Mic | It follows the wake-word setting as today, in the peek and while dozing. Only a deliberate hide closes it. |
| 5 | Delivery | One PR, after #138 PR B. |

## Design

**1. One idle state, in the page.** `app/static/index.html` gets
`let idleSt='full'` (`'full' | 'peek' | 'doze'`), driven from `frame()`.
Every 500 ms (not every frame) it checks `idleCheck(now)`.

- **Not idle** while any of these is true:
  - `busy`, `playing`, `rec`, `transcribing` or `follow` (he's working,
    speaking or listening);
  - `menuOn`, `pendingAppr`, a password card, `ask`, `setEl`, `panelEl`,
    `capOn`, `notifyRow` or the chip's `act` (something is open or showing);
  - `AM` (an agent mark), or `attnPend` while under 60 s old;
  - `body.hover` or `body.bar` (idle never applies in those modes);
  - `dragged` or `downAt` (you're dragging him);
  - `hiddenNow` (a deliberate hide).
- **Idle time** is
  `now - Math.max(lastActive, lastMouse, idleHoldUntil)`. `lastMouse`
  already updates on `mousemove` over his window (`:2661`).
- **States:**
  - **peek** when idle time ≥ `idle_shrink_s` and `idle_mode` isn't `off`;
  - **doze** when idle time ≥ `idle_doze_s` and `idle_mode` is `doze`.
- **`restore(holdMs)`** is called by the events in §3. It sets
  `idleHoldUntil = now + holdMs` and returns to `full` at once.

**2. The peek: a CSS shrink inside the same window.** The window is never
resized (NVIDIA flicker, `window.py:934`).

- **Shrink:** `body.peek #stage{transform:translate(var(--px),var(--py)) scale(.4)}`.
  `#stage` already animates its transform (`.65s`, `index.html:27`), the same
  way hover mode does (`:26`).
- **Corner:** `--px`/`--py` push him toward the corner of his 340×330 stage
  nearest the closest screen corner (±102 px x, ±99 px y). The host passes
  that corner as `window.nixiCorner('br'|'bl'|'tr'|'tl')`, from the monitor
  geometry it already tracks (`window.py:414-470`); the default is `br`.
- **In the peek:**
  - the orbs, rays and halo fade to 25 %, and the caption, chip and cue are
    hidden;
  - the agent mark stays visible, scaled with him, so a pending question is
    still seen;
  - `frame()` keeps running, but skips the orb/spark update (`ORBS`) for
    less work.
- **Clicks:** `sendRegion()` (`:2617`) treats `body.peek` like the notch.
  It always sends a tight region, the peek's box plus 8 px, even when
  click-through is off, so the rest of the 340×460 window lets clicks
  through. It is sent again on `#stage` `transitionend`.
- **Hovering** over the peek for 300 ms, or clicking it, calls
  `restore(12000)`. A click doesn't also do its normal action (open the
  menu); it only brings him back.

**3. What brings him back** (all call `restore(hold)`):

| Event (`onEvent` / page) | Hold | Pops up from doze? |
|---|---|---|
| You: `ev.t==='wake'` ("Hey Nixi"), `nixiCmd` (push-to-talk, hotkeys, `nixi-companion listen`), a click or hover on him | 15 s after the turn ends | yes, with focus (your own action) |
| Prompts: `approve`, `askpass` | until answered, then 12 s | yes, with focus (needs typing) |
| `attention` with `kind!=='done'` | 20 s | yes, **without** focus |
| `remind`; a calendar `ambient` with `ev.alert` | 12 s | yes, without focus |
| `notify` (when `notifyDrain` shows a row); `attention` `done`; `nudge` | the row's 5 s + 6 s | yes, without focus, except in `SIT.fullscreen` or `SIT.meeting`: then they stay in the peek or doze |
| `ambient` without an alert, `situation`, weather, looks | — | no |

While `busy` he stays `full` anyway (§1), so a long answer never shrinks.

**4. Doze: hidden but awake.**

- **Entering doze:** the page sends `native('doze:hide')`, and `window.py`
  hides the window **without** `nixiHidden()`. That means no `/api/sleep`,
  so the wake word, the notification takeover and agent events keep
  arriving. `hiddenNow` stays `false`; `dozing=true` is a separate flag.
- **Leaving doze:**
  - **Without focus:** `native('doze:show')` runs `show_all()` and
    `_apply_region()`, but not `present()`, `grab_focus()` or
    `nixiShown()`. Then `entrance()` plays the pop-in.
  - **With focus** (your own action or a prompt): the existing `show()`.
- **The desktop-notification fallback** in the attention branch (`:1626`)
  stays keyed on `hiddenNow` (a deliberate hide), not on `dozing`.
- **Toggling** (`nixi-companion` / the show-hide key): `toggle()`
  (`window.py:780`) already shows him when the window isn't visible, so
  calling him while he dozes brings him back with focus. Nothing to change
  there.
- **CPU:** while dozing the page stops scheduling `frame()` and wakes on the
  next event. The GTK redraw tick (`window.py:397`) doesn't fire while the
  window is unmapped; the plan checks this.

**5. Settings.** `app/server.py`:
- adds `"idle_mode": "shrink"` to the `CFG` defaults;
- puts it in `/api/state`;
- accepts it in `/api/settings` only as one of `off`, `shrink` or `doze`.

The settings page gets a three-way control under Look, "When idle: stay ·
shrink · shrink, then hide", with the short line "Pops back up when
something happens". `docs/GUIDE.md` gets a short "When idle" section.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Resizing the layer surface:** it flickers on NVIDIA/WebKit
  (`window.py:934`), and window sizing also handles panels and cards.
- **Auto-docking to the notch:** the window slides across the screen and
  the melt animation plays without being asked. That's distracting, and it
  clashes with where you put him.
- **Reusing hide/`nixiHidden` for doze:** it puts him to sleep, so he can't
  pop up. This is the bug the intent describes.
- **Doze on by default:** not yet tested on NVIDIA or multiple monitors.
  Shrink-only is the safe default.
- **Timings as settings:** more UI for little gain. They're constants and
  can become settings later.

## Risks

- **Invisible click-eaters:** a stale or oversized region could block clicks
  near him. The region is resent on every transition and at `transitionend`,
  and checked live with click-through off.
- **Focus theft:** only the three cases with focus may use `show()`; every
  other pop-up must use `doze:show`. Checked live by typing in another window
  while an alert arrives.
- **Flicker on doze hide/show** (NVIDIA): the window is mapped and unmapped,
  not resized. Checked live. If it flickers, doze stays opt-in and gets a
  known-issue note.
- **A peek that never comes back:** the restore list must cover every event
  with a visible effect. A missed event still shows up in the peek (agent
  mark, sound), and hovering or clicking always restores him.
- **Multiple monitors and follow-focus:** the corner is re-sent whenever he
  moves monitor.
- **Hover mode and the notch:** idle is disabled in both (§1), so they don't
  interact.

## Verification

- **Unit tests:** `idle_mode` defaults to `"shrink"` and the settings
  endpoint rejects any value other than the three
  (`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q`).
- **Page logic (headless Chrome against a sandbox server):**
  - after 45 s idle he shows `body.peek`, and the region is tight;
  - an `attention` event restores him and holds 20 s;
  - a `notify` in fullscreen leaves him in the peek;
  - with `doze`, after 5 min `doze:hide` is sent, and an event sends
    `doze:show` (not `show`).
  - The timers are shortened through a test hook (`window.nixiIdleScale`).
- **Live on p620 (the native window):**
  - peek and restore screenshots;
  - clicking a terminal right next to the peek reaches the terminal;
  - typing in another window while a Codex "finished" pops him up from doze
    keeps your typing in that window;
  - doze pop-up on NVIDIA without flicker;
  - while dozing, the server CPU stays at its idle level (`top`).
