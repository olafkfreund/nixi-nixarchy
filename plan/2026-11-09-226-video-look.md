# Plan: a watching look, and YouTube in Chrome counts as video

Issue #226. Intent and spec folded into this plan at the user's request (2026-11-09: "Yes, both", "Small TV with a
play button"). Builds on #209 part 4 (`media_kind`, `Ambient.video`) and #208 (the boombox and the `music`
sustained reaction).

## Why

Watching YouTube in Chrome, Nixi shows the music boombox. Chrome and Chromium give no `xesam:url` over MPRIS, so
#209's URL check can't see a video, and the page draws nothing for `video` at all.

## Decisions (approved by the user, 2026-11-09)

1. **Chrome/Chromium YouTube is video** when a Playing row has no URL, its player name starts with `chromium` or
   `chrome`, and a Hyprland client whose class contains `chrome` or `chromium` has a title that contains that row's
   track title and the word "YouTube". Only the boolean leaves the server; no title is kept, published or logged.
2. **A watching look:** a small TV with a play button, original art in the style of the boombox (no YouTube logo or
   wordmark), shown while `video` is on, under the same rules as the boombox (Reactions on, `look_music` on, not
   quiet). The boombox never shows at the same time (`music` is already false while `video` is true).

## Steps

1. **Server** (codex). In `Ambient.sense`, after `music, video = media_kind(lines)`: if not `video`, for each
   Playing row with no URL from a `chromium*`/`chrome*` player, read that player's title
   (`playerctl -p <name> metadata title`) and `hyprctl clients -j` (once per sense, only in this case, with the
   existing `_run` timeouts); a match per decision 1 sets `video, music = True, False`. A missing `hyprctl`
   (no Hyprland) or any error leaves the result unchanged.
   Tests in `tests/test_situation.py` (`Ambient.run`/`_run` mocked): Chrome row + "… - YouTube - Google Chrome"
   window with that title → video; same window without "YouTube" → music; a YouTube window whose title is another
   video → music; no hyprctl → music; nothing about the title reaches `situation()`.
2. **Page** (codex). `SIT.video` from the situation event; a `video` entry in `sitOn` gated by `info.look_music`;
   on → `RXS.add('video')` (sustained, like `music`), off → delete; a `tv` prop in `REACT.props` drawn beside him
   where the boombox sits, flat colours from the existing palette, inside the 340x460 box at every character.
   Tests in `tests/react_fit.mjs`: video on shows the TV and no boombox; video off hides it; quiet (meeting,
   fullscreen) hides it; the TV stays in the box and off the eyes for all characters.
   → verify: `cd tests && python3 -m unittest test_situation test_media_playing test_react_fit test_character_fit`.
3. **Review, deploy, check** (lead): Codex PR review until clear; nixi-companion bump; p620 and razer; a screenshot
   with YouTube playing in Chrome shows the TV, not the boombox.
