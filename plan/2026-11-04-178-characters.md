# Plan: choose a different body for Nixi

## Decisions (from the approved spec, so this file stands alone)

All code is in olafkfreund/nixoarchy-snowflake, mostly in
`app/static/index.html` (call it the page). Line numbers are approximate:
re-find them by name. Work in a new worktree, `../nixoarchy-snowflake-178`,
on branch `feat/178-characters` from `origin/main`. The artwork reference is
in nixi-nixarchy at `mockups/178/agy-characters.html` and
`mockups/178/agy-notes.md`. The code map is `mockups/178/code-map.md`.

1. **The DOM shape stays.** CSS rules and readers select direct children
   of `#body`:
   - `#body>.ball` and `#body>ellipse` (CSS ~32, 76–89: notch, goo, pop-out,
     bar, bar-done);
   - `#body>path.ball` and `#body>.nix` (~1200);
   - `#body > .ball` in `cardPlace` (~2662) and the bubble's `geo()` (~3641).

   No wrapper group goes inside `#body`. Every character fills the same
   elements:
   - `path.ball` and `#rim`: the silhouette `d`;
   - `g.ball.nix`: art behind the eyes;
   - the two gloss `ellipse`s: shown for Snowflake only;
   - a new direct child, `g.ball.bfront`, after `#face` and before
     `#tears`: art in front of the eyes.

   `.nix`'s width stays "his reach". The glint and frost are Snowflake only.
2. **`CHARACTERS` registry.** Four ids: `snowflake` (the default),
   `terminal`, `robot`, `devil`. Each entry has:
   - `label`;
   - `ball` (`d`);
   - `back` and `front` (fixed built-in SVG strings);
   - `gloss` (bool);
   - `faceBox` `[x, y, w, h]`;
   - `anchors` `{head, face, neck, base, left, right}`, as offsets from
     Snowflake's (Snowflake's are all `[0,0]`, `face` is always `[0,0]`);
   - `pivot` (Snowflake 76);
   - `shadow` (Snowflake 62);
   - `fits` (a set of skin and sit ids);
   - `touch` (a class or `null`).

   `setCharacter(id)` sets the two `d`s, replaces the children of `.nix`
   and `.bfront`, toggles the gloss, and sets `BODY`. Snowflake's strings
   are exactly what `snowPath()` and `nixLambdas()` build today.
3. **The eye rig is never changed.** That's `EYE = {rx:23.5, ry:40.5,
   cx:27.5, cy:-4.5, tilt:.157}` and `R=76`, plus moods, blink, gaze and the
   talk swell. The art is refitted around it, keeping agy's look. Eye
   extremes: x ±4…±51 and y −52…+43, plus gaze.
   - **Terminal:** the screen covers at least x ±60, y −58…+50, and the
     prompt is below y +46.
   - **Robot:** the visor covers the same window, and the LED cheeks are
     outside x ±56.
   - **Devil:** the brows are above y −56, the grin and fang below +46, and
     the cheeks outside ±56.

   Art that must stay clear of the eyes is marked `class="avoid"`.
4. **Anchor offsets go on a new wrapper `<g>` outside** each `SIT_P` group
   (`put()` and `lift()` rewrite those transforms every frame, ~1785–1790),
   outside `SP2.wob` (~1776) and outside `SP2.halo` (~1778). They're created
   once in `drawSit` and `drawSkin`. `PROP_BOX` entries shift by their
   anchor's offset. Anchor use:
   - `head`: skins, hats, `PROP_BOX.head`, the weather slot;
   - `face`: glasses, shades, headphones;
   - `neck`: scarf, tie;
   - `base`: `PROP_BOX.base`, the shadow;
   - `left` and `right`: `PROP_BOX.left` and `.right`, chat, feed.

   The squash pivot is `BODY.pivot`, and the shadow width is `BODY.shadow`.
5. **Fit limits for new art:**
   - within y −120…+100 and x ±96 (below `#cue`, `#agentMark` and `#mirMk`
     at about (8, −110)/(8, −90));
   - the headphone cups clear the body, or headphones aren't in `fits`;
   - the head-cloud weather slot follows `anchors.head`.
6. **The touch:** CSS `@keyframes` (`cursor-blink`, `antenna-pulse`,
   `tail-swish`), with `transform` and `opacity` only.
   - **Paused under `body.still`.** `body.still` is set in the existing
     500 ms `idleCheck()` (~3531–3556) when all of these hold:
     `performance.now() - Math.max(lastActive, lastUser) > 60000`, not
     `idleActive()`, and not `busy`. It's set with
     `classList.toggle('still', bool)`, and only when the value changes.
   - **No animation** under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
7. **Colours:**
   - from `--body0..2`, through per-character gradients in the shared
     `<defs>`: the terminal's case and stand, the robot's shell, the
     devil's body and wings;
   - fixed: the screen and prompt, the visor and antenna light, the horns
     and fang. Each fixed accent has its own dark outline (`--rim`), and
     contrast is at least 3:1 between each accent and its outline or
     background.
8. **The setting:**
   - `look()` in `app/server.py` (~266–280) accepts `character` in the four
     ids, defaulting to snowflake, and drops other values;
   - `state_info` (~5206) sends it;
   - Look gets a Character row of four previews before Skin, with a voice
     hint that changes nothing;
   - `miniNixi()` (~1162–1175) draws from the registry and the live `EYE`.
     It keeps `data-part="body"` on the silhouette.
9. **Not in this issue:** plugin characters. No per-character eye rig.
