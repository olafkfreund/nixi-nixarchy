# Plan: #209 part 1, the quiet gate

The first of the four plans under the approved #209 spec, in its order. It adds no art and no new sense: it makes
everything unasked wait while you're busy, so every later part and #208's reactions can rely on it.

## Decisions carried over from the approved spec

- **Do Not Disturb comes from the shell.** The notifications IPC Nixi already calls (`Inbox.call`, as for
  `activateKey`) answers `isDnd` with `"on"` or `"off"` (the shell's `Service.qml`, `isDnd()`). `Ambient.sense` asks
  at most every 10 seconds; no answer keeps the last value; the first value is `off`. `situation` gains `dnd`.
- **One rule, checked twice.** A reminder or an unasked reaction shows only while none of DND, the lock, fullscreen or
  a meeting is on:
  - on the server, in `Nudge.tick`, before it publishes (today it checks only the lock, and the meeting for speech);
  - on the page, in `nudgeDrain`, when it shows one, so a nudge already queued when DND starts can't slip out. The
    page's own unasked pokes (the failed-unit `confused`) use the same check. #208's reactions will call it too.
- **Agent speech:** while DND is on, `agent_say_ready` returns `{"speak": False, "reason": "dnd"}`, beside the
  meeting check. An agent's "finished" already doesn't talk. **Decided by the user (beyond the spec):** an
  agent summary is unasked speech too, so the same answer for the lock and fullscreen (`"locked"`,
  `"fullscreen"`), checked on the server and again just before the page plays it. Without this, a summary can
  speak over a film or at a locked machine.
- **The page's other unasked reactions** follow the rule as well: the snow `excited` poke, the new-generation
  `t_beam` poke and burst, an agent's "finished" sound and `t_beam` poke (its mark and text line still show; the
  spec's recommendation: silent, the caption still shows), and the `restore` that brings him out of a doze for a
  nudge or an ambient alert. A calendar alert's text line still shows; its `restore` and bar flash wait.
- **Never gated:** approval requests (`/api/approve`, the page's `ask` path), answers to something you asked, and
  boards you open.
- **The calm hold:** a held reminder keeps its place. Today every offer goes stale after 10 minutes. A reminder
  carries a deadline instead: none for break and water, which wait for the next allowed moment, and its window's
  end for a workout (part 3 passes it). Other kinds keep the 10 minutes. The `nudge` event carries what's left,
  so the page enforces the same deadline.
- **The breakpoint stays** (the user's decision): an unlock, a workspace switch, or 20 seconds without Hyprland
  events. No keyboard or compositor idle signal is added.
- Thresholds and new senses are parts 2-4, not here.

## Steps

1. **Server** (coder), `app/server.py`.
   - `Ambient`: a class field `dnd = False` and `dnd_due = 0.0`. In `sense()` (`1652`), when `proactive` is on and
     `t >= dnd_due` (whatever `proactive` says, since agent speech reads it too): `dnd_due = t + 10`; `r = Inbox.call("isDnd")` (`Inbox.call`, `2218`, timeout 1); `on`/`off`
     set `dnd`; anything else keeps it. Add `"dnd"` to `situation()`'s keys (`1649`).
   - `Nudge.tick` (`2091-2123`): return early, keeping `pending`, while `Ambient.locked`, `Ambient.dnd`,
     `Ambient.fullscreen` or `Ambient.meeting` is on (today only `locked`, `2101`). Keep the meeting speech check
     at `2117` as it is.
   - Deadlines: `offer(kind, text, spoken, t=None, until="default")` (`2028`) stores a deadline as the third tuple
     item instead of the offer time: `"default"` means `None` for `break` and `water` and `t + 600` for any other
     kind; a number is a monotonic deadline (part 3 passes a workout window's end). The stale sweep (`2099`) drops
     only entries whose deadline is set and past. The published `nudge` gains `wait`: seconds left, or `null`.
   - `agent_say_ready` (`1203-1213`): after the meeting check, `dnd`, then `locked` and `fullscreen`, each with its own reason.
   - Tests in `tests/test_nudge.py` (its existing harness; `Inbox.call` mocked, never the real IPC): `isDnd` read at
     most every 10 s, unknown answers keep the last value, `dnd` in `situation`; `tick` publishes nothing under DND,
     fullscreen or a meeting and keeps the item; it publishes after they clear at the next breakpoint; a `break` held
     for 30 minutes still shows, with `wait: null`; a `cpu` offer still goes stale after 10 minutes; an offer with a
     numeric `until` is dropped after it; DND is polled with `proactive` off too; `agent_say_ready` says `dnd` (and
     `locked`, `fullscreen`); `/api/approve` is unchanged under DND. `test_speech_budget_and_meeting`
     (`test_nudge.py:98-109`) expects a nudge to publish in a meeting; it now expects it held, and published silently
     only after the meeting ends.
   → verify: `cd tests && python -m unittest test_nudge` and the suite without `*_fit`.
   Traps: import `server` only under `nixi_env`'s throwaway HOME (#176); `Inbox.call` must never reach the real
   shell in a test; `proactive` off keeps clearing `pending` as today.

2. **Page** (coder), `app/static/index.html`.
   - `SIT` (`2415`) gains `dnd:false`; the `situation` handler (`2317`) sets `SIT.dnd=!!ev.dnd`.

   - The `nudge` handler (`2316`): `restore(11000,false)` only when `!quietNow()`; the event's `wait` becomes

   - The other unasked reactions check `quietNow()`: the snow poke (`2318`), the failed-unit `confused` poke (`2319`),
