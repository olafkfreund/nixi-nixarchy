## Approved decisions

### The Flux contract (#57)

- Snapshot `state` (only the newest is kept; short transitions can be skipped) has these new fields:
  - per device: `call {state ringing|talking, caller, number, since}`, `missedCall {caller, number, at}` (10 min) and
    `dnd {on bool|null, by phone|sync|set|connect, at}`
  - top level: `approvals [{kind sudo|ssh, device, since}]`
- `plugins` adds `dnd`. The existing ones are `findmyphone`, `share` and `sms`.
- New method: `dnd.set {device, on}` → `{sent: true}`.
- With no `approvals` key, Flux predates #57: calls, meeting silence and the approval caption stay off.

### The client: `app/phone.py`

- **Socket checks.**
  - The path is `$FLUX_SOCKET`, else `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/flux/fluxd.sock`, else `/run/user/<uid>/flux/fluxd.sock`.
  - `os.lstat` must show a socket (not a symlink) owned by our uid.
  - The parent folder must be owned by our uid and not group- or world-writable.
  - `SO_PEERCRED` must give our uid.
- **Threads.** One reader thread only reads. One worker runs `tick` and the handlers. `Phone.call` waits 10 s. A 30 s
  read timeout is only a switch check, and reconnects back off 1–30 s.
- **The fixed call list:** `ring share.url share.text share.files clipboard.send sms.refresh sms.send dnd.set`. No
  other code opens the socket.
- **`share.files`** accepts only realpaths in `Phone.outgoing`, files Nixi registered: regular files, not symlinks.
- **Records** are keyed device+since (call), device+at (missed), device+kind+since (approval) and id (transfer).
  `seen` survives reconnects, and records already present at Nixi's first snapshot are marked seen silently.

### Features

- **Settings.** `phone_on`, default off, in a Mail-style row with one status line:
  - "Connected: …"
  - "Flux isn't running"
  - "Update Flux for calls and meeting silence"
  - "Turn on Sync Do Not Disturb on the {name} for meeting silence"

  Switching it off runs `phone_reset()` (Mail-style): it closes boards and drafts, bumps `PHONE_GEN`, deletes the
  `outgoing` files and closes the socket.
- **Find my phone.**
  - A named device rings; one capable online device rings; several give a chooser card (`/api/phone/ring {id}`, token
    only).
  - Offline gives "isn't connected, last seen N min". `not_supported` gives "can't ring".
- **Send.**
  - A screenshot is `capture("window")`, copied to `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/nixi-companion/phone/<random>.png` (0600),
    registered, then sent as `share.files`.
  - A single http(s) URL on the clipboard goes as `share.url`, other text as `share.text`, and an image like the
    screenshot.
  - A link or text gives "Sent to your {name}."
  - A file's transfer gives done "On your {name}.", `waiting` "Waiting for your {name}…" and failed "That didn't
    reach…".
  - Files are deleted at the transfer's end or after 10 min.
- **Status.**
  - A `gauges` board, one per device (battery, charging, online or last seen). It may stay in Back.
  - `phone_battery` nudge, 1800 s cooldown: when charge goes from ≥15 to <15 while not charging. It re-arms on
    charging or ≥20.
  - Plugin read `phone-status` = `[{name, online, battery, charging}]`, refused with `network: true`, and part of the
    cache key.
- **Calls.**
  - `Ambient.phone_call` = any `call`. It is in `situation()` and the page's `SIT` and `quietNow()`, and `meeting` is
    not reused.
  - When it turns true: `Speaker.stop()`, cancel follow-up, end `Listen`, and hold wake.
  - Speech gates take it next to `meeting`.
  - An unseen ringing record gives one line, "Anna is calling on your {name}" or "A call on your {name}". It is the one
    exception to the call quiet. It is not said when locked, in a meeting, under Do Not Disturb or with `proactive` off.
    A record first seen as talking isn't announced.
  - Flux's call popup stays. Inbox marks it `phone_call` (app = a Flux device name, title starting "Call from " or
    "Missed call from ", Phone connected to #57). Marking happens before details are blanked, and `notifyIn` skips
    category speech for it.
  - An unseen missed call with a number on an sms device gives a "Missed call from …" card with Text back. With no
    number, no button. It is held while locked, in a meeting, under Do Not Disturb or in a call.
- **Messages.**
  - `sms.refresh`, then a `phone_sms` board of up to 5 threads, `mail_scrub`bed (best effort), read through the mail
    speech path with a playback re-check (lock, meeting, call).
  - The board is excluded from Back, and `phone_on_screen()` joins the `/api/capture` refusal. It is true while a
    messages board, a draft card or a missed-call card is up.
  - The draft `PHONE_DRAFT {id, device, number, name, created, gen}` keeps the recipient on the server, with editable
    text.
  - `/api/phone/send {draft, text}` needs `click_ok(X-Nixi-Click)`, a live draft (<600 s, current gen) and text of
    1–1000 characters. The draft is consumed atomically before `sms.send`.
  - The worker follows the outbox: "Sent.", "Still waiting…" after 30 s, or "That text didn't go."
  - No `notification.reply`.
- **Agent guard.** `click_only` gains `_is_flux`: any `flux-cli`, or `flux` followed by
  `send|clip|url|sms|ring|ping|notify|share`, or `fluxd.sock`. It is checked on raw, cleaned and expanded text. The JS
  twin `bridge/send-policy.js` gets the same rule and shared cases.
- **Meeting silence.**
  - `Phone.meeting_now()` = Meeting Mode's flag, or a timed `cal_items` entry with a meeting link where `start-60s ≤
    now < end`.
  - At the start, for online `can.dnd` devices with `dnd.on == false`: `dnd.set on`, wait ≤10 s for the `by: "set"`
    report, and record its `at` in `silenced`.
  - At the end: `dnd.set off` only where `dnd` is still on, `by: "set"`, with the recorded `at`.
  - `dnd` absent or null is never touched. `silenced` lives in memory only.
- **Approval caption.** An unseen record gives "Approve on your {name} (sudo|ssh)". It isn't spoken, has no buttons,
  and closes when the record goes.
- **Text.** Every line is in `app/lang/{en,nb,pl}.json`.

## Rollback

Switch `phone_on` off (the default), or revert the PR. Nothing is stored outside memory except the setting, and no
existing behaviour changes while it is off. The policy twin PR reverts on its own; it only adds refusals.
