## Decisions (from the approved spec)

- **The umbrella** stays in `#sitFront`:
  - bigger, about 110 units across;
  - a domed canopy with an underside arc, three ribs and a ferrule;
  - tilted about 15° over his head and offset left, with a shaft down to
    the **right foot of his top lambda** (≈ (64, −118)), where a J-hook
    curls round it;
  - it rotates about that grip by `w*.45`, clamped to ±6°;
  - the canopy is within about 75 units of the grip.
- **The `#wx` layer** is a direct child of `#guy`, before `#cam`.
  - **Rain:** 8 streaks (12 when heavy), 14×2.4, `#9fd3ff`, 12° slant (22°
    when windy, downwind), about 220 u/s (×1.4 when heavy); opacity
    `0.85·sin(π·p)`; every fall stays inside x ±145.
  - **Snow:** 6 flakes, r 2–3.5, opacity 0.55–0.9, about 22 u/s, weaving
    `sin(t·0.9+i)·6`.
  - **Halved under lowFx.** Hidden in bar, hover, calm and goohide.
  - **Reduced motion:** 3 still particles.
- **Looks:** `sitOn.snow`, `cold1` and `cold2` are added, with the 3 s / 8 s
  rule, all mapped to `look_weather`. Rain and snow exclude each other at
  once.
- **Top zone:** party hat > umbrella > beanie > nightcap, only with no
  skin. The scarf yields to the tie and to a `base` plugin prop.
- **Wind:** a spring `G.wind` with target `lean` while windy, else 0.
  - his body tilt gets `+4°·G.wind`;
  - hat tops, the canopy and the scarf tail move downwind;
  - nothing leans under reduced motion.
- **Shiver:** at `cold ≥ 1`, while idle, about every 25 s: 0.6 s of ±1.5
  units sideways on `#body`. None under reduced motion.
- **Server:**
  - **The place:** `now`, else `home`, whichever has coordinates. Its key is
    the coordinates rounded to 2 decimals.
  - **The gate:** Open-Meteo only with `proactive`, `ambient_weather` and
    `look_weather` all on.
  - **The mapping:** codes 51–67, 80–82 and 95–99 → `rain`; 71–77 and 85–86
    → `snow`; heavy = 65, 67, 82.
  - **Validation:** temperatures −80…60, wind 0…400, direction 0…360;
    anything else becomes `None`.
  - **Precedence:**
    1. a good Open-Meteo reading for the current key, under 2 h old;
    2. else the `omarchy weather` reading (`feel = temp`, wind from the
       number, `dir = None`, `heavy = false`);
    3. else nothing.
  - **Margins:**
    - `cold`: 0→1 at ≤ 5, 1→2 at ≤ −5, 2→1 at > −3, →0 at > 7;
    - windy on at ≥ 25 km/h, off at < 20;
    - `lean`: ±1 when `|sin(radians(dir))| > 0.3`, else 0;
    - the margins reset on a key change and when the gate goes off.
  - **Refreshes:** `location_act`, a place-key change seen in `sense()`, and
    `/api/settings` changes to `proactive` or `look_weather` all poke
    `Ambient`. The poll stays 1800 s.
  - **`/api/state`** gains `weather: {sky, cold, windy, lean, heavy, feel,
    wind, source}`.
- **Day/night** isn't used in this slice.

## Traps that apply to every step

- **The `//` trap.** In the page script, a `//` comment runs to the end of
  the line: no code after one. Inside `<style>`, only `/* */`.
- **GPU safety (hard rule).**
  - Never open the page in a desktop browser.
  - Tests and previews run only in headless Chromium with `--headless=new
    --disable-gpu --use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader
    --disable-gpu-compositing`.
  - After each run, `journalctl -k --since -10min | grep -Ei 'ring
    .*timeout|gpu reset'` must print nothing.
  - No filters, no blur, transform and opacity only.
- **`frame()` writes only when needed.** The body looks run inside
  `if(!(off&&sitSettled))` (`:1509-1510`). The `#wx` layer is driven
  outside it. New body looks (scarf, beanie) must be added to `off`'s
  checks.
- **`put(el,x,ax,ay,rot)`** (`:1507`) rotates about `(ax,ay)`. A positive
  rotation moves a top item's top to screen right, but swings a hanging
  item's tail to screen left.
- **The server never makes a network call** from `state_info()` or a
  request handler. The forecast is fetched in `Ambient`'s own thread only.
- **Tests** must never reach the real Open-Meteo: inject `urlopen`, as
  `location_suggest` does (`server.py:1188`).

  breathing changes every frame. It now checks the weather parts only
  (both).
