# Plan: rich notification cards

## Decisions carried over from the approved spec

- **Today first.** `tests/notify_fit.mjs` and `tests/test_inbox.py` pass unchanged before and after. A row with no image
  and no buttons keeps today's row, cut to 120, with today's `notify_details`, secret and one-time-code checks.
- **Shell (patch 903, nixarchy):**
  - a new IPC `delegateRich(ms)` does what `delegate(ms)` does and also sets `richLease`; `delegate(ms)` clears it;
  - only while `richLease` is set are buttoned rows queued and the new fields added;
  - eligibility under `richLease`: every named action can be carried (at most 3, each identifier 1-64 printable
    characters, none of `\x00-\x1f` or `\x7f`); `!row.execActions` stays;
  - the queued row gains `actions: [{id, text}]` (from `actionsOf()`, `text` cut to 40), `image` (`row.image` unless
    it's a `qsimage` URL, else `""`), and `rich` (`String(row.bodyRich).slice(0, 600)`);
  - a buttoned row is held 30 seconds after hand-over, not 12;
  - a new IPC `invokeKey(key, id)` as written in spec §2, which only signals an action the live sender offered.
- **Server (`app/server.py`):**
  - `Inbox.tick` calls `delegateRich`, and `delegate` when that gets no reply; `Inbox.CAP` goes to 1 MiB;
  - today's row is unchanged; a row gets a `card` only when it has an image or buttons;
  - order: `Inbox.runs(rich)` (tags first, then the four entities, only the listed tags, `{t,b,i,u,host}` runs, 600
    characters and 64 runs, `host` only for http/https/mailto, no `href` to the page); then the privacy gate over the
    summary, every run's text and every label, uncut (`notify_details`, `SECRET_LINE`, `Inbox.OTP`); a refused row with
    buttons goes back through `showKey` and isn't published; then the image; then buttons;
  - `NImage`: `O_RDONLY|O_NOFOLLOW|O_NONBLOCK`, `fstat` regular and ≤ 1 MiB, read ≤ 1 MiB + 1 byte, static PNG only
    (signature, `IHDR` 1-2048 each way, no `acTL`), id `secrets.token_hex(8)`, 60 s, at most 8, emptied when
    `notify_details` goes off; `GET /api/nimage/<id>` with `[0-9a-f]{16}`, `image/png`, `Cache-Control: no-store`,
    else 404;
  - buttons: labels and a random `rev` only; `Inbox.acts[key] = (rev, ids, when)`, capped at 64, 30 s, no text;
    `/api/notify/act` takes `how: "action"`, an integer `i` 0-2 (not a bool or string), and `rev`; pops on a matching
    `rev` only, then `Inbox.call("invokeKey", key, id)` once; open, dismiss and expiry pop it too; on `none` the page
    shows "That notification has gone.".
- **Page (`app/static/index.html`):** a card takes the caption row's place: app icon and name, summary, runs (≤ 4
  lines), a 96 px preview, ≤ 3 buttons, "+N"; `createElement`/`textContent` only; links underlined with their host,
  not clickable; marked seen only once drawn, not drawn or marked in notch or `body.bar`; a click on the card is
  `notifyAct('open')`; a button posts its index and `rev` and stops propagation; Esc dismisses; buttons are click-only;
  arrival, sound, envelope and bubble as today; 7 seconds; `sendRegion` covers it.
- **Answered at approval (2026-11-07):** no preview for `qsimage` pixel data for now; today's sound for each kind, the
  bell prop later with the reactions slice.
- Nothing from a card is logged, written to disk or sent to an agent.

The spec's "Open questions" list is garbled (its first item runs into the second): a leftover from an edit. Its
decisions above are unaffected; it isn't edited here.
