# Plan: #209 part 3, breaks

The third of the four plans under the approved #209 spec. It adds a weekly workout schedule, a water reminder and a
way to snooze or dismiss a reminder. It ships with or after #208, because its snooze needs a clickable chip.


## Decisions at approval (2026-11-08)

The user approved this plan on 2026-11-08 ("approve 209 plans with the recommendations"). That accepts the recommendation on every open question:

1. Part 3 builds the reminder chip itself, in #208's style (#208's plan has none).
2. Water is in scope (spec Q3).
3. Snooze offers 10 and 30 minutes, plus Done.
4. Thresholds as written: up to 7 workout windows, including ones that cross midnight; water every 0/45/60/90 minutes.

## Builds on part 1

Part 1 (`plan/2026-11-07-209-1-quiet-gate.md`) must be merged first. This plan relies on: `Nudge.tick` holding
offers while DND, the lock, fullscreen or a meeting is on; `Nudge.offer(kind, text, spoken, t=None, until="default")`,
where `"default"` gives `break` and `water` no deadline and a number is a monotonic deadline (the workout window's end);
the `nudge` event's `wait`, which the page turns into `ev.until`; and `quietNow()` on the page. Part 1's plan already
names `water` as a no-deadline kind. Re-read part 1's merged code before step 1 (its deviations are being applied on
`docs/209-connect-senses-b`).

## Decisions carried over from the approved spec

- **Gate:** `proactive` and `react_break`, as the break is today.
- **Workout schedule.** A new setting `"workout": []`, at most 7 entries of `{"days": [0-6], "at": "HH:MM"}` (Monday
  is 0), validated on save like the other lists. Each entry opens a window from `at` to an hour later, local
  wall-clock time, and offers `workout` once, with that deadline. Overlapping windows give one reminder. "Once a day"
  means shown once: if the window ends while held, it's gone. The day's record is memory only, so a restart inside a
  window can show it again.
- **Water.** A new setting `"water_every": 0` minutes, off by default, or one of 45, 60 and 90. A counter like the
  break's restarts on a lock of 5 minutes or more, and offers `water`.
- **Snooze and dismiss.** Each reminder carries an `id` in its `nudge` event. `POST /api/nudge` with `{"id", "act":
  "snooze" | "dismiss", "minutes": 10 | 30}`, with the page token, acts only on the reminder last shown with that
  `id`; an old chip does nothing.
  - **Snooze** offers it again after that many minutes, past its kind's cooldown. It doesn't reset the break or water
    counter. A workout snooze ends at the window's deadline.
  - **Dismiss** drops a pending copy, ends that workout entry for the day, and restarts the break or water counter.
- **Settings.** Under "Remind me to move": a row for the schedule (day buttons and a time, add and remove) and a
  choice for water. Both settings in `info`, as `react_break` is.
- New offers are visual only (`spoken` false); new kinds go in `Nudge.COOL`, `QUIP_FALLBACK` and `app/quips.json`.
  The break keeps today's spoken line.

## Steps

   - `DEFAULTS` (`120`, the row at `223`): `"workout": [], "water_every": 0`.
   - `/api/settings` (`6613`), after the `notify_speak` check (`6668`): `workout` is accepted only if it is a list of
     at most 7 dicts, each with exactly `days` (a non-empty list of distinct ints 0-6, sorted on save) and `at`
     (`re.fullmatch(r"([01]\d|2[0-3]):[0-5]\d")`); otherwise ignored, as `notify_speak` is. `water_every` is
     accepted only if it is `0, 45, 60, 90` (an int, not a bool).
   - `state_info()` (`6296`): `"workout": CFG.get("workout") or [], "water_every": CFG.get("water_every") or 0`,
     beside `agent_voice_mute` (`6328`).
   - Tests in a new `tests/test_breaks.py` (`_ServerEnv`): good values save; 8 entries, `"24:00"`, day 7, an extra
     key, a bool `water_every` and 30 are refused and leave the old value.
   → verify: `cd tests && python -m unittest test_breaks test_settings_176`.
   Traps: `CFG` lists come back from JSON, so compare as lists; `save_config` merges, so a refused value must not be
   written at all.

2. **Reminders, ids, snooze and dismiss** (codex), `app/server.py`.
   - **One lock.** `Nudge.lock = threading.Lock()`. `tick` holds it for its whole body and `act` (below) for its
     whole body: `/api/nudge` runs on a `ThreadingHTTPServer` thread (`server.py:7333`) while `watch` and `tick` run
     on the ambient thread, and both touch `pending`, `shown`, `snoozed` and `skip_cool`. Two clicks on one id then
     consume it once.
   - `Nudge` fields: `water_since = None`; `workout_done = set()` of `(window start date, key)`, a key being
     `(tuple(days), at)`; `workout_pending = set()` (the same pairs, offered but not yet published); `shown = {}`
     (kind -> `(id, text, until)`); `snoozed = {}` (kind -> `(due, text, until)`); `skip_cool = set()`;
     `next_id = 0`; `last_sched = None` (the schedule last seen).
   - `watch` (`2199`):
     - **Switches off clear state.** With `react_break` off: drop `break`, `water` and `workout` from `pending`,
       `snoozed`, `shown` and `skip_cool`, clear `workout_pending`, set `water_since = None`, and return from this
       part. With `water_every` 0: the same for `water` only. When `CFG["workout"]` differs from `last_sched`: drop
       a pending or snoozed `workout`, its `shown` id, and `workout_pending`; then `last_sched` is the new list.
     - **Unlock edge** (`2203-2206`): where a lock of 300 s restarts `active_since`, it restarts `water_since` too.
     - **Water:** `water_since` starts at `t`; with `water_every` in `(45, 60, 90)` and not locked and
       `t - water_since >= water_every*60`: `offer("water", quip("water"), False, t)`, `water_since = t`.
     - **Workout:** with `now` local (`datetime.datetime.now().astimezone()` when `now` is `None`), each entry gives
       up to two windows: today's (when `now.weekday() in days`) and yesterday's (when yesterday's weekday is in
       `days` and `at + 1 h` crosses midnight, e.g. 23:30). Each window is `[start, start + 1 h)` on aware local
       datetimes (DST shifts it by the clock change, accepted). Open windows whose `(start date, key)` is in
       neither `workout_done` nor `workout_pending`: if any, one `offer("workout", quip("workout"), False, t,
       until=t + (latest window end - now).total_seconds())`, and their pairs go into `workout_pending`.
       `workout_done` drops pairs older than two days.
     - **Snoozed:** for each `snoozed` item with `due <= t`: pop it; drop it if its `until` has passed; else re-offer
       it with the same `until` and add the kind to `skip_cool`.
   - `tick` (`2229`): the stale sweep that drops a `workout` (deadline passed) also clears `workout_pending`, so a
     held workout that never showed is never marked done and isn't offered again (its window has ended). The
     cooldown check (`2242`) skips kinds in `skip_cool`. On publish, a reminder kind (`break`, `water`, `workout`)
     gets `id = next_id` (then `+1`), `shown[kind] = (id, text, until)` (`until` from the pending entry's deadline),
     and the `nudge` event gains `id`; `skip_cool.discard(kind)`. Other kinds publish without `id`, as today. A
     published `workout` moves `workout_pending` into `workout_done`, and also marks done every other entry whose
     window today or yesterday intersects the published window, so a 09:30 entry doesn't follow a 09:00 one
     published at 09:05 ("overlapping windows give one reminder"). A chain (09:00, 09:50, 10:40) gives one reminder
     for each window that doesn't intersect a published one.
   - **Lifecycle:** "shown" means published by the server. The page keeps at most one queued event per kind
     (`nudgeQ` is a `Map` keyed by kind, `index.html:2316`), so a newer publish of a kind replaces the older one
     there and the chip always carries the current id; the page drops a held event at its `until` (part 1). A
     workout published but dropped by the page at its deadline before it was drawn counts as shown, accepted.
   - `Nudge.act(id, act, minutes, t=None)`: find the kind whose `shown` id equals `id`; none returns `False` and
     changes nothing. Pop it from `shown` (a second click does nothing). `snooze`: drop `pending[kind]`, then
     `snoozed[kind] = (t + 60*minutes, text, until)`; for a workout whose `until` comes before the due time,
     nothing is stored (it would be gone). `dismiss`: drop `pending[kind]`, `snoozed[kind]` and the kind from
     `skip_cool`; `break` sets `active_since = t`, `water` sets `water_since = t`; a workout is already done for the
     day. Snooze leaves the break and water counters alone.
   - `POST /api/nudge`, beside `/api/notify/act` (`6562`), so the page token is required as for it (not in
     `TOOL_PATHS`): body `{"id": int, "act": "snooze", "minutes": 10|30}` or `{"id": int, "act": "dismiss"}`
     (`minutes` only for snooze); `id` and `minutes` must be `int` and not `bool`; anything else a 400. Answers
     `{"ok": Nudge.act(...)}`. Under `proactive` off, `False`.
   - `proactive` off (`tick`, `2231`) clears `pending`, `snoozed`, `shown`, `skip_cool`, `workout_pending`, and sets
     `water_since = None`, beside today's `active_since = None`.
   - `COOL` (`2155`) gains `"workout": 0, "water": 0` (their windows, the done-set and the counter pace them).
     Quips: `workout: ["Workout time!", ...]`, `water: ["Time for some water.", ...]` in `QUIP_FALLBACK` and
     `quips.json`.
   - Tests in `tests/test_nudge.py` (its harness; `now` and `t` passed in, nothing waits): an entry for today at
     `now - 5 min` offers once with a deadline at its window end; held by DND past the end, it is gone and not
     offered again, and the entry isn't marked done until a publish; two overlapping entries give one, also when
     the second opens after the first was published (09:00 published at 09:05, then 09:30); a Monday 23:30 entry
     still fires at 00:10 Tuesday and not on a Tuesday-only schedule; tomorrow's day doesn't fire; editing the
     schedule drops a pending workout; water at 45 fires once per 45 active minutes, a 5-minute lock restarts it, a
     2-minute one doesn't, `water_every` 0 drops a pending one; a published reminder carries an `id`, a `cpu` nudge
     doesn't; snooze 10 re-offers once after 10 minutes past the cooldown (several ticks later, still once), drops
     a pending copy and leaves `active_since`; dismiss restarts the break counter and drops a pending copy; an old
     `id` and a second click return `False`; two threads acting on one id give one `True`; `act` during a `tick`
     waits for it; a workout snoozed past its deadline is gone; `react_break` off with a snooze waiting, then on,
     shows nothing old; `/api/nudge` without the token is refused, a dismiss without `minutes` is accepted, `true`
     as an id or minutes is a 400.
   → verify: `cd tests && python -m unittest test_nudge test_breaks`.
   Traps: the workout window is wall-clock, but the deadline handed to `offer` is monotonic (`t + seconds left`);
   a clock change mid-window only shifts the deadline, which is accepted; never read the real clock in a test;
   `BUS.pub` under `Nudge.lock` is fine (it only queues), but nothing under the lock may block on I/O.
